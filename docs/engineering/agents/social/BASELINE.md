## API, library, and data boundary

- Feed query and presentation are split between `lib/feed/feed-posts-query.ts`, `lib/feed/feed-client.ts`, `components/feed/social-feed.tsx`, and `app/api/feed/posts/route.ts`. The query retains compatibility fallback variants for schema drift; this is resilience, not proof that every deployment has the canonical shape.
- Social graph data uses `follows`, `follow_requests`, `account_follows`, `accounts`, and `account_relationships`; messaging uses `conversations`, `messages`, and `message_attachments`; groups use `group_threads`, `thread_members`, `group_messages`, and `group_message_reactions`; notifications use `notifications`, `notification_preferences`, `notification_events`, and `notification_delivery_log`. Evidence is in `docs/engineering/generated/database-schema.md`, `docs/engineering/generated/database-objects.md`, and the service/query paths above.
- Route permission inventory is generated in `docs/engineering/generated/permissions.md`. Static indicators show session/auth or service-role usage on the principal Social routes, but the map explicitly warns that detected markers do not prove resource-level authorization.
- The group and DM handlers perform membership checks before service-role reads/writes: `app/api/groups/threads/[id]/messages/route.ts:57-70,139-152`, `app/api/groups/threads/[id]/members/route.ts:52-65,95-108`, and `app/api/messages/[conversationId]/realtime/route.ts:10-32`.
- The notification preference gate is present in source migrations, not verified as deployed on the approved target: `supabase/migrations/20250210000001_comprehensive_notification_system.sql:568-596`, `supabase/migrations/20260415235824_notification_ecosystem_prefs_rls_outbound.sql:51-112`, and `supabase/migrations/20260416000323_team_comms_fanout_and_rls_cleanup.sql:8-68`. `OptimizedNotificationService.shouldSendNotification` falls back to `true` on RPC failure at `lib/services/optimized-notification-service.ts:728-747`.

## Intended direction

The accepted product decisions in `docs/engineering/DECISIONS.md` are now the sequencing authority: CP-020 requires a standalone feed, CP-021 requires full community groups, and CP-022 requires full realtime messaging. The wider roadmap calls for the WS-1.3 messaging fanout layer, WS-1.6 CI/test recovery, Phase 4 notification-center and navigation unification, CSP/XSS hardening with an E2E payload test, and realtime resilience through a centralized channel/reconnect strategy (`docs/DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md:54-57,95-124,193-200`).

The implementation order is therefore: close live authorization/Realtime evidence for the delivered feed/groups/DM slices; resolve collaboration ownership and group discovery semantics; verify the notification RPC and failure logging on the target; add Social critical-path E2E/security coverage; then schedule scale and unification work.
