# Customers, Support, and Case History

## Objective

Give authorized support staff enough context to resolve commerce issues without exposing unnecessary PII or financial controls.

## Customer Profile

- Identity snapshot.
- Linked Tourify profile.
- Order history.
- Ticket purchases.
- Service bookings.
- Subscription state.
- Refund history.
- Disputes.
- Support cases.
- Risk flags.

## PII Controls

- Mask email by default.
- Reveal only with permission.
- Audit reveal.
- Do not expose full payment method details.
- Limit address visibility to fulfillment need.
- Restrict exports.

## Support Case Types

- Payment failed.
- Duplicate charge.
- Order not received.
- Ticket not issued.
- Refund request.
- Seller not responding.
- Service dispute.
- External checkout problem.
- Subscription billing.
- Account or entitlement problem.

## Case Fields

- Type.
- Priority.
- Status.
- Customer.
- Seller.
- Order.
- Transaction.
- Amount.
- Owner.
- SLA.
- Internal notes.
- Customer-facing messages.
- Resolution category.
- Evidence.
- Audit.

## Support Actions

- Contact customer.
- Contact seller.
- Resend receipt.
- Resend ticket or digital delivery.
- Escalate to finance.
- Escalate to moderation.
- Initiate refund request.
- Link duplicate cases.
- Resolve.

## SLA Views

- Unassigned.
- Assigned to me.
- Due soon.
- Breached.
- Waiting on customer.
- Waiting on seller.
- Waiting on provider.
