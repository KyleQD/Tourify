# Transfer Restrictions, Holds, and Repurchases

Private and exempt-offering securities are often restricted. Phase 4 should default to blocked transfers unless an approved policy explicitly permits them.

## Restriction sources

- offering exemption and holding period;
- investor and purchaser eligibility;
- jurisdiction and state law;
- issuer agreement, right of first refusal, consent, or minimum denomination;
- sanctions, AML, fraud, legal process, divorce, probate, bankruptcy, or tax hold;
- transfer-agent legend and partner restricted list;
- smart-contract wallet eligibility.

## Transfer request workflow

Holder submits transferee and transaction details → partner eligibility review → transfer-agent/legal review → fee and tax disclosure → consent/waiver process → official approval → cash/asset settlement → position reconciliation. No token transfer should bypass this workflow.

## Issuer repurchases

Repurchase programs need board authorization, funding source, pricing methodology, conflict review, disclosure, eligibility, pro-rata or priority rules, tender/withdrawal windows, and partner/counsel approval. Never market issuer repurchases as guaranteed liquidity.

## Rule versioning

Restrictions must be versioned by instrument, offering, holder category, acquisition date, jurisdiction, and effective period. Store the exact rule evaluation and partner decision used for each approval or rejection.
