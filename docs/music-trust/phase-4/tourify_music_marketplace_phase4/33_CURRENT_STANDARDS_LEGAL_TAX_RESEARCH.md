# Current Standards, Legal, and Tax Research

This is a research checklist, not legal advice. Revalidate all sources immediately before implementation and before every live offering. Laws, rules, staff positions, forms, partner permissions, and tax reporting can change.

## Tokenized securities

The SEC's January 28, 2026 statement describes tokenized securities as securities represented through cryptographically secured ledgers and distinguishes issuer-sponsored tokenization from third-party tokenization. Tokenization does not remove the underlying instrument from securities law. The official ownership and rights model must be disclosed.

## ATS and broker-dealer

An ATS must register as a broker-dealer and comply with Regulation ATS. SEC staff FAQs updated December 17, 2025 address disclosures, custody, clearance/settlement, and DLT activities. Tourify should not collect or match securities orders unless the exact activity is performed under an approved registered structure.

## Transfer agents and DLT

SEC staff has stated that a registered transfer agent may use DLT as its official Master Securityholder File or a component, with transaction data potentially on-chain and personal information off-chain. Phase 4 must designate the official record and keep Tourify read models subordinate.

## Offering pathways

Regulation Crowdfunding permits up to $5 million in a rolling 12-month period through one registered intermediary and generally restricts resale for one year. Rule 506(c) permits general solicitation only if all purchasers are accredited and reasonable verification steps are taken. Regulation A Tier 2 permits up to $75 million but requires qualification, audited statements, ongoing reporting, and limits for certain non-accredited investors.

## Restricted securities and resale

Private-offering securities are commonly restricted. Secondary resales require registration or an exemption; Rule 144 conditions and holding periods depend on issuer and holder facts. Product transfer logic must use counsel/partner decisions, not a generic countdown alone.

## Communications, diligence, supervision, and execution

FINRA guidance emphasizes reasonable investigation of private placements, communications standards, supervision, and—where customer transactions are handled—best execution. Offering posts, artist promotions, market data, orders, and partner routing require controlled records and review.

## Sanctions, AML, and money transmission

OFAC obligations apply to digital and fiat transactions. FinCEN treatment depends on facts and circumstances, including acceptance and transmission of value. Use regulated providers and obtain activity-specific analysis rather than relying on labels such as “noncustodial.”

## Tax

The IRS published 2026 Form 1099-DA instructions for reportable digital-asset broker transactions. Separate ordinary royalty income reporting, security distributions, sale proceeds, basis, withholding, entity reporting, and digital-asset reporting. Assign each form and data field to the responsible party.
