# Regulatory Roles and Entity Boundaries

Phase 4 must begin with a written role map for every entity and vendor. Product labels do not determine regulatory status; actual activities do.

## Required role matrix

For each workflow, identify who performs and supervises:

- issuer formation and authorization;
- securities-law pathway selection;
- due diligence and reasonable investigation;
- marketing review and communications approval;
- investor recommendations or non-recommendation controls;
- KYC, customer identification, AML, sanctions, and fraud screening;
- accreditation or investment-limit verification;
- subscription acceptance, escrow, refunds, and closing;
- broker-dealer or funding-portal activity;
- official securityholder records and transfer agency;
- custody and wallet control;
- order receipt, routing, matching, execution, and best execution;
- settlement, cancellation, corrections, and fails;
- distributions, corporate actions, tax reporting, books and records;
- complaints, suspicious activity escalation, and regulatory inquiries.

## Recommended Tourify role

Tourify should initially be a technology, data, and artist-workflow provider. It may provide factual catalog and rights data, approved issuer content, navigation into partner workflows, read-only position displays, and operational case management. It should avoid receiving transaction-based compensation, recommending investments, negotiating securities terms, handling customer funds, holding private keys, accepting orders, or representing that it operates a market unless counsel and registrations expressly permit those activities.

## Entity separation

Use separate legal entities and accounting books where counsel recommends separation among:

- Tourify platform operations;
- issuer or special-purpose entity holding the royalty interest;
- payment and royalty collection accounts;
- regulated intermediary and ATS;
- transfer agent;
- custody provider;
- optional technology subsidiary.

No commingling of artist royalties, subscription proceeds, investor distributions, marketplace revenue, or Tourify operating funds.

## Decision gates

No implementation flag may move to production until the responsible partner is named, contracts are signed, insurance and security reviews are complete, required registrations are confirmed, and the exact Tourify activities have written counsel approval.
