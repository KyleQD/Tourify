# Phase 4 Definition of Done

Phase 4 is complete only when the applicable production scope meets every requirement below with implementation and test evidence.

## Governance and partners

1. The actual Tourify and partner regulatory roles are documented and approved.
2. Every live offering has an approved pathway record.
3. Regulated partner, transfer-agent, custody, tax, sanctions, and payment responsibilities are named.
4. No unsupported Tourify broker, exchange, custody, or money-transmission activity exists.
5. Staff permissions and dual-control approvals are implemented.

## Issuer and offering

6. Eligible interests reference immutable Rights Passport, ledger, and valuation snapshots.
7. Issuer authority, beneficial owners, conflicts, liens, contracts, and data quality are reviewed.
8. Disclosure versions are immutable and trace every public field.
9. Material changes trigger review and required investor action.
10. Offering launch, suspension, closing, withdrawal, and reporting states are controlled.

## Investor and primary transactions

11. Partner onboarding and eligibility scopes are synchronized without unnecessary sensitive data.
12. Investment limits and accreditation results come from the responsible system.
13. Subscription, payment, escrow, cancellation, refund, acceptance, allocation, and closing are idempotent and reconcilable.
14. Tourify does not create a legal position before partner/transfer-agent confirmation.
15. Investor documents and acknowledgements are reproducible.

## Ownership and secondary liquidity

16. The official ownership source is unambiguous.
17. Position reconciliation detects and escalates breaks.
18. Transfer restrictions default deny and are versioned.
19. Secondary orders are controlled by the approved broker/ATS partner.
20. Market data is sourced, timestamped, and labeled for staleness.
21. Executions, settlements, corrections, and cancellations are immutable and reconciled.
22. No liquidity or appreciation guarantee appears in the product.

## Tokenization and custody

23. The system works without tokenization.
24. Any token maps to an executed legal instrument and official position record.
25. Private keys are not stored by Tourify.
26. Contract roles, pause, recovery, migration, and audits are complete.
27. Wallet loss and forced-transfer procedures match the legal record.

## Operations, reporting, and security

28. Issuer reporting and material-event calendars operate.
29. Distributions and tax documents reconcile to Phase 3 and partner records.
30. Communications, promoter activity, and offering posts are archived and approved.
31. Music-specific and market surveillance alerts reach the responsible partner.
32. Complaints and incidents have tested playbooks.
33. RLS and capability tests prevent cross-account access.
34. Financial records use compensating corrections rather than destructive edits.
35. Partner outage, disaster recovery, and kill-switch tests pass.
36. Phase 1 music upload/playback and Phase 2/3 functionality pass regression tests.
37. Legal, compliance, security, finance, and executive launch approvals are recorded.
