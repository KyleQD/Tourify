# Product Model and User Journeys

Phase 4 introduces a Marketplace area without disrupting Tourify's existing music marketplace for ordinary music purchases. Securities, royalty participation interests, fan utility, and music downloads must be separate products with separate labels, terms, ledgers, and checkout paths.

## Marketplace product modes

- **Offering Readiness:** artist prepares catalog, entity, disclosures, valuation, and data room.
- **Primary Offering:** approved investors subscribe through the regulated partner.
- **Portfolio:** investor views partner-synchronized positions, distributions, documents, and restrictions.
- **Transfer Request:** holder requests a permitted transfer, repurchase, or partner-routed sale.
- **Secondary Market Access:** eligible users enter the partner ATS or embedded partner workflow.
- **Issuer Reporting:** artist or issuer publishes approved updates and financial/royalty reports.

## Artist journey

1. Select an eligible certified catalog or defined royalty interest.
2. Complete issuer-entity and authority verification.
3. Select a proposed financing goal without selecting the legal exemption in code.
4. Build a data room from Rights Passport, revenue, contracts, recoupment, valuation, and risk records.
5. Submit for Tourify preflight, counsel, and partner due diligence.
6. Resolve deficiencies and lock an immutable disclosure version.
7. Launch only after the partner supplies an approved offering identifier and status.
8. Monitor subscriptions, closing conditions, distributions, reporting deadlines, and holder communications.

## Investor journey

1. Discover an offering through the permitted communication mode.
2. Review the complete risk summary before any economic projection.
3. Create or link the regulated partner account.
4. Complete identity, sanctions, tax, eligibility, accreditation or investment-limit checks as required.
5. Acknowledge illiquidity, transfer restrictions, loss risk, music-industry concentration, and valuation uncertainty.
6. Submit a subscription through the partner.
7. Receive closing, allocation, position, and restriction records.
8. View distributions and reports in Tourify after partner reconciliation.
9. Request a transfer or enter partner-operated secondary trading only when eligible.

## Experience principles

- Never display “buy token” before required disclosures and partner eligibility.
- Never blend royalty investment performance with streaming popularity or fan leaderboards.
- Use “estimated valuation range,” not “token value.”
- Display cash flow, rights scope, term, deductions, concentration, restrictions, and partner source timestamps.
- Treat pending partner events as pending; never optimistically create legal positions.
