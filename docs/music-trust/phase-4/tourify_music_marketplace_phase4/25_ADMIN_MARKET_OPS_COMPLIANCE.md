# Admin Marketplace Operations and Compliance

Admin capabilities must be separated by role and supported by complete evidence trails.

## Operational queues

- issuer eligibility and data-room deficiencies;
- partner due diligence and filing status;
- disclosure and communication approval;
- onboarding/eligibility exceptions;
- subscription, escrow, refund, and closing breaks;
- transfer-agent position breaks;
- order/execution/settlement exceptions;
- distributions and tax-document reconciliation;
- market surveillance and fraud alerts;
- complaints, legal notices, and incidents;
- overdue issuer reporting.

## Role separation

Separate issuer operations, investor support, financial operations, compliance, legal, security, and platform administration. Sensitive approvals require dual control. No engineer or ordinary admin should be able to alter positions, executions, eligibility, or compliance outcomes directly.

## Case management

Every case records source, severity, owner, SLA, evidence, communications, partner escalation, legal hold, decisions, approvals, remediation, and closure. Cases may create holds but must not erase source financial records.

## Dashboard controls

Show partner uptime, webhook lag, unreconciled cash and positions, stale market data, failed jobs, open surveillance alerts, overdue filings, document expirations, key/certificate health, and feature-flag status.
