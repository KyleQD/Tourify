# Complaints, Disputes, and Incident Response

Investor complaints and market incidents need a different process from ordinary music-content support.

## Complaint categories

- misleading offering or promotion;
- subscription, refund, allocation, or fee dispute;
- incorrect position or distribution;
- unauthorized account, wallet, order, or transfer;
- eligibility or sanctions decision;
- market-data or execution complaint;
- issuer reporting failure;
- rights/copyright dispute affecting the instrument;
- privacy, security, or identity incident.

## Routing

Determine whether Tourify, the issuer, broker/funding portal, ATS, transfer agent, custodian, payment provider, tax provider, or counsel owns the response. Preserve a unified user-facing case while maintaining partner-specific regulatory records.

## Incident playbooks

Create playbooks for partner outage, compromised account, webhook forgery, incorrect mass allocation, settlement break, wallet key compromise, contract exploit, chain outage/fork, leaked offering document, sanctions hit, market manipulation, issuer default, and material rights dispute.

## User protections

Provide acknowledgement, status updates, escalation, evidence upload, accessible appeal, privacy protection, and final written response. Do not expose investigation details that compromise surveillance or other users.
