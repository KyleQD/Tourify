# Communications, Promotions, and Social Controls

Tourify's feed and artist promotion tools cannot be treated as ordinary social media when they discuss securities offerings.

## Approved communication library

Every offering gets a controlled library of approved facts, risk statements, images, links, disclaimers, and expiry dates. Artist, staff, affiliate, and promoter communications must use the approved version appropriate to the exemption and offering status.

## Prohibited content

- guaranteed returns, appreciation, liquidity, or streaming growth;
- “safe,” “risk-free,” “copyright-backed,” or “AI-proof” claims without qualification;
- cherry-picked revenue without periods, deductions, or source;
- unapproved valuation targets;
- fake scarcity, countdowns, or social proof;
- undisclosed compensation or investor status;
- testimonials implying typical performance;
- public solicitation where the selected exemption prohibits it.

## Feed controls

Offering posts should be a distinct content type with partner-approved disclosures, audience restrictions, jurisdiction controls, archiving, edit/version history, comments policy, and review queue. Ordinary feed resharing must not strip the required context.

## Promoter records

Store promoter identity, relationship, compensation, approval, content version, distribution channels, dates, and audience. Suspension of an offering must disable associated calls to action globally.
