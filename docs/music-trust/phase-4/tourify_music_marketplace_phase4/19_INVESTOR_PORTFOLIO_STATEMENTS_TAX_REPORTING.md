# Investor Portfolio, Statements, and Tax Reporting

The portfolio is a synchronized reporting surface, not the official custody account or tax system unless a responsible registered partner expressly designates it so.

## Portfolio display

Show instrument, issuer, official quantity, acquisition date, restriction status, partner/custody source, distributions, pending corporate actions, cost/basis status supplied by the responsible party, and last synchronization time.

## Statements

Provide immutable monthly/quarterly statements or partner links containing opening position, activity, executions, fees, distributions, withholding, closing position, pending settlements, restrictions, and reconciliation status. Corrections create replacement versions.

## Tax boundaries

Determine responsibility for royalty income forms, partnership/corporate reporting, withholding, security-sale proceeds, cost basis, and digital-asset broker reporting. As of tax year 2026, Form 1099-DA applies to certain broker-facilitated digital-asset dispositions; do not assume Tourify is or is not the reporting broker without tax counsel and partner mapping.

## Export and retention

Allow secure exports with document hashes, source provider, generation date, and correction history. Retain according to applicable securities, tax, privacy, and litigation-hold requirements.
