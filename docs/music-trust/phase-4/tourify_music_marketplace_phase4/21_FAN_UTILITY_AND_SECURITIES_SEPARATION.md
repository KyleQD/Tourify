# Fan Utility and Securities Separation

Tourify may offer fan collectibles and access benefits, but those products must not be used to disguise or market an investment interest.

## Separate systems

Maintain separate product IDs, contracts, checkout, terms, wallet assets, accounting, tax treatment, marketing, UI badges, and support queues for:

- music purchases and licenses;
- fan collectibles/access passes;
- loyalty rewards;
- royalty participation securities;
- ordinary subscriptions.

## No economic leakage

A fan collectible should not receive royalty distributions, catalog sale proceeds, redemption tied to financial performance, profit-sharing, or marketing centered on appreciation unless it enters the regulated instrument path.

## Bundling controls

Any bundle containing a security and fan benefits must be approved by counsel and partner, itemized, clearly disclosed, and handled within the regulated offering workflow. Never let a merch purchase automatically create an investment position.

## Terminology

Reserve “invest,” “security,” “royalty participation,” “position,” and “return” for regulated products. Use “collectible,” “access,” “benefit,” and “supporter item” only where accurate.
