# Market Surveillance, Manipulation, and Abuse

Music communities create unique manipulation risks because artists, managers, fans, promoters, and investors can coordinate both streaming activity and security trading.

## Risk scenarios

- wash trading or self-trading;
- matched orders and coordinated fan-group trading;
- pump-and-dump promotion;
- false streaming campaigns intended to influence valuation or price;
- undisclosed paid promotion;
- insider trading around releases, sync placements, disputes, takedowns, or catalog sales;
- account takeover and unauthorized orders;
- multiple accounts evading limits;
- quote stuffing or abusive cancellation where the venue permits orders;
- manipulation through artificial marketplace purchases or social metrics.

## Surveillance responsibilities

The partner venue owns regulated market surveillance and suspicious-order review. Tourify contributes music-specific signals, communication records within its systems, account-link intelligence, catalog events, and streaming-anomaly flags under a documented data-sharing and escalation agreement.

## Alert workflow

Alert → triage → evidence preservation → partner escalation → restriction/hold decision → investigation → resolution → regulatory or legal reporting by the responsible party. Keep investigation access highly restricted and preserve chain of custody.

## Conflicts

Monitor employees, artists, managers, labels, promoters, reviewers, and affiliates. Maintain restricted lists, blackout periods, personal-trading policies, preclearance where required, and disclosure of Tourify/partner compensation.
