# Issuer Reporting and Ongoing Disclosures

Closing an offering begins, rather than ends, the issuer's reporting responsibilities.

## Reporting calendar

Store pathway-specific deadlines, annual/semiannual/current reports, royalty statements, distribution reports, financial statements, material-event notices, tax filings, amendments, holder votes, and termination filings.

## Material events

Require escalation for catalog sale, major license, distributor or administrator change, rights dispute, takedown, infringement claim, artist incapacity/death, bankruptcy, default, covenant breach, material revenue correction, data breach, sanctions issue, smart-contract incident, or suspension of distributions/trading.

## Source synchronization

Approved issuer reports should derive from Phase 3 reconciled ledger periods and Phase 2 rights snapshots. If underlying records change after publication, create a restatement workflow; do not mutate the filed report.

## Delinquency controls

Missed reports trigger reminders, admin cases, investor notices, partner escalation, offering/trading holds, and public status changes according to the governing documents and partner policy.
