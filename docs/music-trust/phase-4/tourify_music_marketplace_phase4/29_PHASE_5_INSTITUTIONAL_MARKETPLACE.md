# Phase 5 Institutional Marketplace Readiness

Phase 5 may expand only after Phase 4 demonstrates reliable primary offerings, position reconciliation, reporting, and controlled secondary access.

## Potential expansion

- multi-venue routing and best-execution analysis;
- institutional catalog portfolios and funds;
- qualified-buyer or institutional-only products;
- standardized security identifiers and reference data;
- issuer-sponsored auctions and tender programs;
- collateral and lending integrations;
- cross-border offerings with jurisdiction modules;
- regulated API access for advisers, brokers, labels, and administrators;
- portfolio risk, stress testing, and benchmark products.

## Requirements before expansion

Demonstrated market surveillance, audited controls, accurate tax and cost-basis reporting, robust issuer reporting, partner redundancy, legal analysis for every jurisdiction, formal model-risk governance, liquidity-risk metrics, and an incident history acceptable to partners and insurers.

## Still prohibited by default

Permissionless security trading, anonymous investors, algorithmic market making by Tourify, leverage, derivatives, lending, rehypothecation, or cross-chain bridges without new legal, security, and partner approvals.
