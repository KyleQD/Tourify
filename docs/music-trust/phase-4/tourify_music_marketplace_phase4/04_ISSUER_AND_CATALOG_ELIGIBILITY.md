# Issuer and Catalog Eligibility

Only rights interests that are legally defined, operationally measurable, and financially reconcilable should enter the marketplace pipeline.

## Minimum catalog requirements

- active Rights Passport with no material unresolved dispute;
- clear master/composition/administration scope for the interest offered;
- payor and collection-account verification;
- at least 12–24 months of reconciled revenue unless the partner approves a different standard;
- complete recoupment, advances, liens, distribution, label, publishing, sample, and licensing records;
- source statements tied to the Phase 3 ledger;
- no ownership allocation based solely on current editable claims;
- a defined offering term, territory, revenue categories, deductions, and termination rules.

## Issuer requirements

- verified legal entity and beneficial owners;
- board/member/manager authorization;
- tax identification and banking readiness;
- good-standing and bad-actor review;
- conflicts and related-party disclosures;
- bankruptcy, litigation, lien, and enforcement disclosures;
- authorized representative and backup signer;
- covenant to deliver ongoing source statements and material updates.

## Eligibility scoring

Use a private readiness score for workflow prioritization, never as a public investment recommendation. Dimensions should include rights certainty, revenue coverage, data quality, concentration, recoupment completeness, legal readiness, reporting capability, operational continuity, and unresolved exceptions.

## Automatic holds

Block progression for ownership disputes, unverified authority, missing payor chain, unexplained revenue gaps, sanctions hits, unresolved copyright notices, unsupported valuation data, material contract inconsistencies, or partner rejection.
