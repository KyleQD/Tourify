# Offering Structuring, Disclosures, and Data Room

The offering data room must be a versioned evidence system, not a folder of editable marketing files.

## Instrument definition

Define precisely:

- legal issuer and obligor;
- asset or contract generating cash flows;
- percentage or formula represented;
- gross versus net basis and permitted deductions;
- seniority and recoupment waterfall;
- start, end, maturity, extension, and termination;
- territories, revenue categories, exclusions, and reserve policy;
- voting, information, audit, consent, and enforcement rights;
- transfer restrictions and repurchase rights;
- treatment of catalog sale, distributor change, dispute, takedown, artist death, bankruptcy, or force majeure.

## Disclosure set

Include approved versions of:

- offering memorandum/Form C/Form 1-A or partner-required disclosure;
- Rights Passport and chain-of-title summary;
- historical royalty statements and reconciliation report;
- valuation methodology and assumptions;
- issuer financial statements;
- material contracts and redaction log;
- risk factors specific to streaming, platforms, artist conduct, AI, copyright, samples, concentration, fraud, and illiquidity;
- conflicts, fees, compensation, affiliates, and use of proceeds;
- technical token/custody description if applicable;
- tax discussion and investor suitability limitations;
- ongoing reporting calendar.

## Version control

Every public fact must point to a signed disclosure version. Material changes create a new version, partner review, investor notice, reconfirmation or cancellation workflow, and a preserved prior version. Marketing content may only reference fields approved for that disclosure version.

## Data room controls

Use document-level permissions, watermarked downloads, access logs, retention rules, legal holds, virus scanning, short-lived signed URLs, and explicit public/private/confidential classifications. Never place confidential contracts or investor data on-chain.
