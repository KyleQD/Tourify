# Runbook — Institutional Complaints and Incidents

## Intake

Record in `music_institutional_complaints` with subject refs and evidence hashes only.

## Severity

- Critical: NAV/ownership mismatch, unauthorized transfer, custody key exposure (must not exist in Tourify)
- High: classification bypass, MNPI leak, settlement break
- Medium: stale partner sync, UX conflation with download marketplace
- Low: labeling/documentation

## Response

Open reconciliation exception if needed; dual-control kill switches; compensating corrections only; close with partner reference.
