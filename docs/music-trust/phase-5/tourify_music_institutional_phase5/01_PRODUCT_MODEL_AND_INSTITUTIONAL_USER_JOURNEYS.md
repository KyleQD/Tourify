# Product Model and Institutional User Journeys

## Institutional products

### Catalog Exchange

A controlled workspace where an artist, label, publisher, estate, or rights owner can prepare a catalog for a direct sale, partial assignment, administration mandate, license, or structured transaction. Tourify coordinates data, diligence, bids, documents, and closing evidence but does not determine legal classification.

### Institutional Data Room

A permissioned evidence room containing Rights Passport snapshots, registrations, agreements, royalty statements, reconciliations, disputes, recoupment obligations, chain-of-title documents, tax and entity documents, technical assets, and approved valuation materials.

### Underwriting Workspace

A professional workspace for buyer analysts and approved advisers to review revenue quality, rights certainty, counterparty risk, concentration, decay, fraud indicators, disputes, contractual restrictions, scenarios, and valuation ranges.

### Fund and SPV Administration View

A synchronized read/write workflow for approved fund administrators and advisers covering commitments, subscriptions, capital calls, NAV periods, positions, waterfall inputs, distributions, investor reports, and document delivery. The official accounting books remain with the appointed administrator.

### Institutional Portfolio

A read model for approved institutions showing verified catalog exposure, revenue sources, rights categories, territories, duration, concentration, valuation versions, actual-versus-forecast performance, cash distributions, restrictions, and data-quality confidence.

## Seller journey

1. Select eligible tracks, works, releases, royalty streams, or catalog entity.
2. Choose a proposed transaction goal without declaring its legal classification.
3. Complete authority, entity, rights, dispute, encumbrance, and exclusivity questionnaires.
4. Create a frozen diligence snapshot from Phase 2 and Phase 3 records.
5. Upload missing evidence and resolve blocking discrepancies.
6. Receive an eligibility and readiness report.
7. Invite an approved adviser, counsel, intermediary, or buyer group.
8. Approve data-room access and confidentiality terms.
9. Receive IOIs, bids, or partner-managed subscriptions.
10. Negotiate terms through versioned term records.
11. Execute legal documents outside or through approved signature providers.
12. Complete closing, payment, transfer, registration, and post-closing reconciliation.

## Institutional buyer journey

1. Join or create an institutional organization.
2. Complete partner-managed identity, authority, eligibility, and compliance checks.
3. Obtain role-limited access to opportunities.
4. Sign confidentiality and data-use terms.
5. Review the approved data-room snapshot.
6. Submit diligence requests and structured findings.
7. Build an internal underwriting case and investment-committee package.
8. Submit a nonbinding IOI or approved bid.
9. Complete partner-led order, subscription, or closing process.
10. Receive official ownership/position confirmation from the approved recordkeeper.
11. Monitor portfolio and reporting through synchronized Tourify views.

## Artist experience rule

Institutional workflows must never make ordinary artist upload, profile playback, fan listening, EPK, feed sharing, or marketplace downloads harder. Institutional eligibility is an optional catalog-capital capability layered onto certified music.
