# Private Funds, SPVs, and Adviser Boundaries

## Supported vehicle records

Tourify may synchronize records for:

- single-asset SPVs;
- multi-catalog SPVs;
- 3(c)(1) or 3(c)(7) private funds where approved by counsel;
- co-investment vehicles;
- feeder or parallel vehicles;
- continuation or tender vehicles;
- securitized-asset vehicles only in a separately approved workstream.

## Tourify boundary

Tourify must not:

- choose investments for a fund;
- allocate capital among opportunities using discretion;
- recommend investments to investors;
- calculate official NAV or maintain official partnership books;
- hold subscriptions, capital-call proceeds, or fund assets;
- determine investor eligibility without the designated partner;
- act as general partner, investment adviser, administrator, custodian, trustee, or auditor.

## Required partner map

Each vehicle records the legal entities and providers responsible for:

- sponsor/general partner/manager;
- investment adviser and registration/exemption status;
- broker/placement agent/intermediary;
- fund administrator;
- bank/escrow/custodian;
- auditor;
- tax provider;
- transfer agent or recordkeeper;
- legal counsel;
- valuation provider;
- blockchain/tokenization provider, if any.

## Vehicle documents

Track versioned governing documents, PPM/offering documents, subscription documents, side letters, management/advisory agreements, administration agreements, custody/bank agreements, valuation policy, expense-allocation policy, conflicts policy, audit records, and investor reporting calendar.

Because private-fund rules and reporting requirements change, active requirements must be sourced from current counsel and providers rather than copied permanently into product code.
