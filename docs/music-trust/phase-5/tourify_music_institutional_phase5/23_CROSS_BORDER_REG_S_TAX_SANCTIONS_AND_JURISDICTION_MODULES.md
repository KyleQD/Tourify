# Cross-Border, Regulation S, Tax, Sanctions, and Jurisdiction Modules

## Default boundary

Phase 5 launches U.S.-first with jurisdiction allowlists. Cross-border access is blocked until counsel, partners, tax providers, privacy review, sanctions controls, and product rules approve each jurisdiction and transaction type.

## Jurisdiction module

Each module defines:

- permitted seller, issuer, buyer, investor, adviser, and intermediary types;
- offering and resale pathway;
- marketing and website-access restrictions;
- investor eligibility;
- transfer and holding restrictions;
- required disclosures and language;
- KYC/AML, beneficial ownership, and sanctions controls;
- tax withholding and reporting;
- currency and payment rails;
- data residency and privacy;
- consumer/investor complaint process;
- licensing and record-retention requirements.

## Regulation S controls

Where counsel approves concurrent offshore and U.S. offerings, enforce offshore-transaction and directed-selling controls, U.S.-person restrictions, distribution-compliance periods, legends, and resale restrictions through the approved intermediary.

## Tax

Tourify does not determine tax treatment. Store provider-calculated classifications, withholding rates, treaty claims, forms, allocations, and document references. Do not put tax forms in ordinary tables.

## Sanctions

Use approved providers and current lists. Sanctions, ownership, geographic, and wallet screening must support ongoing monitoring, not only onboarding.
