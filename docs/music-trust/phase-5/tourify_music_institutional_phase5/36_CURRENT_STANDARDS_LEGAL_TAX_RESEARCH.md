# Current Standards, Legal, Regulatory, and Tax Research

**Research date:** July 17, 2026  
**Purpose:** architecture and issue spotting only; not legal, investment, accounting, or tax advice.

## Tokenized securities

The SEC staff’s January 28, 2026 statement describes issuer-sponsored and third-party tokenized-security models and emphasizes that tokenization is a record format for a financial instrument that remains subject to federal securities law. Phase 5 therefore keeps legal rights and the official securityholder record above any optional token representation.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities

The SEC’s March 17, 2026 interpretation addresses federal securities-law application to certain crypto assets and transactions. Product counsel must review the final instrument and transaction rather than assuming a token label changes legal treatment.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/03/s7-2026-09

A December 2025 SEC statement described a DTC preliminary tokenization program in which DTC’s systems track tokenized entitlements on official books and records. Phase 5 treats depository/transfer-agent models as provider-controlled integrations.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/peirce-121125-tokenization-trending-statement-division-trading-markets-no-action-letter-related-dtcs-development

## Private funds and advisers

SEC small-business resources updated April 24, 2026 describe 3(c)(1) and 3(c)(7) private-fund structures, common Regulation D capital-raising pathways, and adviser registration/exemption considerations. Tourify must not create a fund or adviser role through software defaults.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/private-funds
- https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/starting-private-fund

The SEC private-fund-adviser page warns that the Fifth Circuit vacated the 2023 private-fund adviser rules in June 2024. Do not encode the vacated quarterly-statement, audit, adviser-led-secondary, restricted-activity, or preferential-treatment rules as currently effective solely because older implementation guides describe them. Existing Advisers Act, custody, antifraud, fiduciary, contractual, state, and other requirements still need current counsel analysis.

Source: https://www.sec.gov/investment/private-fund-advisers

## Form PF

The SEC and CFTC extended the compliance date for previously adopted Form PF amendments to October 1, 2026. In April 2026 they also proposed further amendments that could change reporting obligations. Phase 5 should use configurable reporting exports and a monitored rules register, not hardcoded forms.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2025/09/s7-22-22
- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/04/s7-2026-13

## Private placements and institutional execution

FINRA’s 2026 oversight report reiterates reasonable-investigation, communications, supervision, filing, suitability/Reg BI, conflicts, and evidence expectations for private placements. Phase 5 data rooms and diligence logs are designed to support—but not replace—the responsible broker-dealer’s obligations.

Source: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-finra-annual-regulatory-oversight-report/private-placements

FINRA’s 2026 best-execution guidance states that firms receiving customer orders retain best-execution responsibilities and need regular and rigorous review where applicable. Tourify therefore does not operate an order router by default; it synchronizes approved partner routing and execution evidence.

Source: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-finra-annual-regulatory-oversight-report/best-execution

## Investor categories

The SEC’s 2020 accredited-investor rule also expanded categories eligible to qualify as QIBs under Rule 144A. A May 2026 petition asks the SEC to amend qualified-purchaser and QIB definitions, but a petition is not an adopted rule. Do not hardcode institutional thresholds or assume a proposed change is effective; rely on current partner/counsel determinations.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2020/08/accredited-investor-definition
- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/05/petition-rulemaking-amend-definitions-qualified-purchaser-qualified-institutional-buyer

## Custody

Current Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-2 addresses custody of client funds and securities and qualified custodians. SEC staff and Commission activity regarding crypto-asset custody continued through 2025–2026. Phase 5 requires custodian and counsel approval rather than assuming self-custody or a wallet provider satisfies custody obligations.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/ia-2176.htm
- https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/trading-markets-121725-statement-custody-crypto-asset-securities-broker-dealers
- https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/peirce-statement-custody-crypto-assets-093025

## Cross-border

Regulation S provides non-exclusive safe harbors for qualifying offshore offers, sales, and resales, with conditions and resale restrictions. It does not create a generic global passport. Phase 5 uses per-jurisdiction modules and blocks cross-border activity by default.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/divisions/corpfin/internatl/foreign-private-issuers-overview.shtml
- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/1998/02/offshore-offers-sales-regulation-s-effective-date-60-days-after-publication-federal-register

## Valuation

SEC valuation guidance for regulated funds emphasizes governance, pricing-service diligence, methodologies, testing, and oversight. Although institutional music vehicles may have different legal structures, Phase 5 adopts similar model-risk principles and assigns official valuation/NAV responsibility to the legally responsible party.

Sources:

- https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2020-302
- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/valuation-guidance-frequently-asked-questions

## Tax and accounting

Fund, SPV, direct catalog sale, royalty assignment, license, debt, tokenized security, partnership allocation, withholding, and cross-border structures can produce materially different tax and accounting outcomes. Tourify should store provider results and document references rather than determine treatment. Tax forms, beneficial-owner evidence, and bank data belong with approved providers or highly restricted storage.

## Required monitoring

Before implementation and each launch, re-check:

- SEC rulemaking and interpretations;
- FINRA rules and annual oversight guidance;
- adviser, fund, custody, Form PF, and private-placement requirements;
- transfer-agent, depository, ATS, broker, and tokenization guidance;
- IRS and jurisdictional tax changes;
- sanctions and AML requirements;
- privacy, cybersecurity, and data-residency rules;
- DDEX and music-rights standards relevant to catalog evidence.
