# Securitization and Structured-Finance Readiness

## Status

Disabled by default. This document defines prerequisites, not authorization to launch.

## Potential future structures

- royalty-receivable notes;
- asset-backed securities;
- tranched catalog cash-flow vehicles;
- borrowing-base facilities;
- warehouse facilities;
- credit-enhanced or insured structures.

## Additional parties and controls

A securitized product can require issuer/SPV counsel, arranger, underwriter or placement agent, trustee, custodian, account bank, backup servicer, administrator, auditor, tax counsel, rating agency, verification agent, transfer agent, and approved trading/settlement infrastructure.

## Data requirements

- asset eligibility and concentration tests;
- receivable-level histories;
- obligor/platform exposure;
- delinquency, dispute, adjustment, and collection timing;
- servicing standards;
- cash-control accounts and waterfall;
- triggers, covenants, reserves, and events of default;
- bankruptcy-remoteness and true-sale analysis;
- investor reporting and surveillance.

## Prohibitions

No tranching, leverage, rating display, collateral pledge, note issuance, or automated liquidation may be enabled without a new legal, accounting, tax, security, and partner approval package.
