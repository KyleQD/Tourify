# Institutional Portfolio Analytics and Risk

## Portfolio dimensions

Provide institution-configurable analytics for:

- master versus composition exposure;
- artist, songwriter, label, publisher, distributor, administrator, platform, genre, territory, currency, and vintage concentration;
- revenue-source and counterparty concentration;
- duration, reversion, termination, consent, and renewal exposure;
- catalog age, decay, volatility, seasonality, and release dependence;
- playlist, viral, social, and sync concentration;
- unresolved rights, missing registrations, and data-quality confidence;
- recoupment, liens, reserves, and collection delays;
- AI/provenance, fraud, and streaming-manipulation indicators;
- valuation, liquidity, and exit assumptions.

## Risk snapshots

Risk snapshots are immutable, dated, model-versioned, and linked to holdings and source data. They must distinguish observed facts from model estimates.

## Stress scenarios

Examples:

- loss of a major DSP or distributor;
- platform royalty-rate reduction;
- territory or currency shock;
- catalog decay acceleration;
- playlist removal;
- artist controversy or publicity event;
- rights dispute or termination claim;
- delayed collections;
- counterparty failure;
- fraud restatement;
- licensing or AI-policy change.

## Limits and alerts

Institutions can define internal limits and alerts, but Tourify does not make discretionary trades or rebalance portfolios. Alerts are informational and routed to authorized users.
