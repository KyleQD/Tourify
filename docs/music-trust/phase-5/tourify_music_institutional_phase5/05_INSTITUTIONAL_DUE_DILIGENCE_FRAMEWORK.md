# Institutional Due Diligence Framework

## Diligence workstreams

### Rights and chain of title

Verify acquisition, assignment, work-made-for-hire, songwriter, producer, label, publisher, administrator, sample, neighboring-rights, and termination/reversion records.

### Revenue and accounting

Verify statements, source files, reporting periods, currencies, deductions, reserves, recoupment, unmatched usage, duplicate lines, adjustments, related-party revenue, and cash receipts.

### Catalog and audience

Review release history, decay, listener concentration, platform dependence, territory exposure, playlist dependence, social conversion, ticket/merch correlations, fraud indicators, and catalog durability.

### Legal and contractual

Review disputes, notices, litigation, audit rights, consent rights, change-of-control clauses, MFNs, reversion, termination, exclusivity, union/guild obligations, and privacy/publicity rights.

### Operational and counterparty

Assess distributor, publisher, administrator, DSP, collection society, label, manager, and service-provider concentration and continuity.

### Technology and provenance

Review source files, fingerprints, Human-Origin evidence, AI disclosures, Content Credentials, data integrity, and access history.

## Request and finding model

Diligence requests must have owner, due date, severity, confidentiality level, requested evidence, response version, reviewer, and resolution state. Findings must be linked to the exact source evidence and may be:

- informational;
- condition to bid;
- condition to closing;
- valuation adjustment;
- representation/warranty request;
- indemnity/escrow request;
- disqualifying issue.

## No silent overrides

A red flag can be resolved, accepted, priced, insured, or waived only through a versioned decision by the authorized institution and required partner. Tourify administrators cannot silently clear substantive rights or investment issues.

## Evidence standard

Each completed diligence workstream records who reviewed it, what evidence was used, review date, unresolved exceptions, and whether the conclusion can be reused or must be refreshed.
