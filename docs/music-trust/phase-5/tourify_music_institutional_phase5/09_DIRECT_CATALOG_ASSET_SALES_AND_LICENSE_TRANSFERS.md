# Direct Catalog Asset Sales and License Transfers

## Direct transaction workspace

Phase 5 must support direct catalog transactions separately from securities offerings. The workspace can coordinate:

- purchase and sale agreements;
- copyright assignments;
- master, composition, royalty, administration, or neighboring-rights assignments;
- exclusive and non-exclusive licenses;
- retained rights and carve-outs;
- notices, consents, registrations, and collection-direction changes;
- purchase-price allocation inputs;
- transition and servicing instructions;
- post-closing revenue reconciliation.

## Closing checklist

Closing cannot be marked complete until the approved closing agent confirms:

- executed transaction documents;
- authority and signatures;
- required third-party consents;
- payment or escrow status;
- asset schedules and identifiers;
- transfer/assignment deliverables;
- registration or recordation instructions;
- distributor, publisher, administrator, PRO/CMO, MLC, SoundExchange, and other notices as applicable;
- effective date and revenue cutover;
- post-closing servicing and reporting responsibilities.

## Revenue cutover

The royalty ledger must support pre-close, stub, and post-close periods. Do not rewrite historical ownership. Create effective-dated rights and allocation snapshots and preserve seller/buyer reconciliation adjustments.

## No automatic legal transfer

A Tourify status change, database row, token transfer, or payment confirmation does not independently transfer copyright or contractual rights. The executed legal instruments and required registrations/notices control.
