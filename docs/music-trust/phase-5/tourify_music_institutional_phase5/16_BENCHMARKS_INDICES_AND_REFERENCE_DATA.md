# Benchmarks, Indices, and Reference Data

## Purpose

Institutional users need comparison tools, but music catalogs are illiquid and heterogeneous. Phase 5 may produce research benchmarks only after methodology, licensing, governance, and conflict review.

## Potential benchmark dimensions

- master/composition revenue growth;
- catalog age and vintage;
- genre and territory;
- revenue volatility and durability;
- platform and collection-channel mix;
- transaction multiples where permitted;
- yield and cash-conversion periods;
- valuation confidence and rights quality.

## Methodology requirements

- published methodology and eligibility rules;
- source-data rights and licensing;
- survivorship and selection-bias controls;
- outlier and restatement handling;
- constituent and weighting governance;
- review frequency;
- conflict disclosures;
- backfill policy;
- version history;
- prohibition on presenting modeled benchmarks as investable indices without separate approval.

## Reference identifiers

Support optional LEI, ISIN, CUSIP or other identifiers only through licensed/authorized sources. Never fabricate identifiers. Tourify IDs remain internal stable keys.

## Display controls

Every benchmark display states methodology version, observation window, sample size, confidence limitations, and whether transaction data is indicative, appraised, or executed.
