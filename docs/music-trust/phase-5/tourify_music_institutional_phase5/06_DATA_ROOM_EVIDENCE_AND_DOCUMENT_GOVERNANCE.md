# Data Room, Evidence, and Document Governance

## Data-room principles

- private by default;
- organization- and deal-scoped access;
- least privilege and document-level restrictions;
- immutable document versions and hashes;
- visible watermarking for sensitive downloads;
- short-lived signed URLs;
- download, view, print, and export controls;
- complete access logging;
- automatic revocation at mandate or NDA expiration;
- legal hold and retention support.

## Document categories

- organizational and authority documents;
- chain-of-title and acquisition agreements;
- songwriter, producer, performer, label, publisher, administrator, and distribution agreements;
- registrations and identifier records;
- royalty statements and bank/fund-admin confirmations;
- tax, audit, valuation, and financial materials;
- disputes, notices, consents, liens, and encumbrances;
- technical audio/source evidence and provenance;
- offering, fund, or transaction documents;
- closing and post-closing evidence.

## Snapshot and disclosure sets

A data room can contain live working documents, but each IOI, bid, subscription, IC decision, and closing must reference a frozen disclosure set. The system must identify documents added, removed, superseded, or materially changed after each frozen set.

## Confidentiality controls

Support NDA status, clean-team access, buyer conflict screens, restricted-person lists, artist-sensitive materials, privileged-document exclusion, and export review. Privileged legal advice should not be uploaded as ordinary transaction evidence.

## Watermarking

Generated watermarks should include viewer organization, user, deal, document version, and timestamp. Watermarks are deterrence and evidence, not a substitute for access control.
