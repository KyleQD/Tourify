# Multi-Venue Routing and Execution Quality

## Scope

Only applies where an approved broker or adviser routes institutional securities orders or transaction interests among eligible venues. Direct asset-sale auctions remain separate.

## Tourify role

Tourify may aggregate partner status and execution-quality data but does not receive, prioritize, route, or execute customer securities orders unless counsel and registrations explicitly permit it.

## Data model

- parent order or transaction intent;
- approved broker/intermediary;
- eligible venues;
- quotes or indications;
- routing decisions supplied by partner;
- execution timestamps, quantities, prices, fees, and venue;
- rejected/canceled states;
- settlement status;
- execution-quality review and exceptions.

## Best-execution evidence

Where applicable, the responsible broker must retain the basis for venue selection, price, size, liquidity, timing, fees, likelihood of execution/settlement, conflicts, and review results. Tourify can display synchronized evidence and exceptions.

## Conflicts

Disclose routing payments, affiliate venues, principal capacity, inventory interests, issuer compensation, and other material conflicts supplied by partners.

## No synthetic consolidation

Do not create a misleading “national best bid and offer” or claim consolidated liquidity for illiquid private instruments without a legally and methodologically valid source.
