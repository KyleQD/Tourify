# Phase 6 — Global Licensing and Clearance Exchange Readiness

Phase 6 may extend the verified catalog and institutional infrastructure into a global licensing, sync, sampling, brand, creator, venue, and media-clearance exchange.

Potential scope:

- machine-readable license availability and restrictions;
- sync, master-use, mechanical, sample, interpolation, neighboring-rights, and territory workflows;
- quote, negotiation, approval, contract, cue-sheet, usage, billing, and royalty tracking;
- buyer-side music supervision and agency workspaces;
- AI-training and model-output licensing as a separate opt-in product;
- enterprise rights APIs and DDEX-aligned exchange;
- cross-border collection and tax modules;
- rights conflict and clearance insurance integrations.

Phase 6 must not begin until Phase 5 proves institutional identity, data-room security, transaction classification, rights snapshots, cash reconciliation, partner controls, and operational resilience.
