# Testing, Pilot, and Rollout

## Testing layers

### Unit

Classification gates, role/authority checks, snapshot hashes, bid and auction state machines, integer/rational calculations, waterfall traces, risk metrics, versioning, and reconciliation.

### Database and RLS

Cross-organization isolation, deal-room access, document scope, fund/admin roles, append-only records, official-source constraints, security-invoker views, and service-role boundaries.

### Integration

Phase 2 rights snapshot → Phase 3 revenue/valuation → Phase 4 offering/position → Phase 5 data room, transaction, fund, NAV, and reporting workflows.

### Partner sandbox

Identity/eligibility, transfer agent, fund administrator, bank, custodian, execution venue, tax, and signature flows with duplicate, late, out-of-order, missing, and corrected events.

### E2E

Seller readiness, institutional onboarding, NDA/data room, diligence, underwriting, IOI, bid, direct closing, fund commitment, capital call, NAV import, distribution, secondary transfer, and incident handling.

### Regression

Upload, preview, publishing, marketplace downloads, feed sharing, Jukebox playback, library entitlements, EPK, analytics, mobile playback, Phase 2 certificates, Phase 3 ledgers, and Phase 4 investor workflows.

## Pilot

Begin with a small number of high-quality catalogs and one transaction pathway at a time. Prefer a direct catalog sale pilot before fund, secondary, tokenized, or cross-border products.

## Rollout gates

- approved role map and contracts;
- clean audit and security review;
- successful reconciliation over multiple cycles;
- tested kill switches and rollback;
- operations training;
- insurance and incident readiness;
- no unresolved critical rights or financial exceptions.
