# Indications of Interest, Bids, Auctions, and Negotiation

## Supported processes

- confidential bilateral negotiation;
- nonbinding indication of interest;
- sealed-bid auction;
- multi-round auction;
- best-and-final-offer round;
- issuer/seller tender or repurchase;
- partner-operated securities subscription or secondary order process.

## IOI data

An IOI should identify proposed price or range, transaction type, rights scope, currency, financing assumptions, diligence conditions, exclusivity request, closing timing, validity period, required approvals, and whether it is binding.

## Bid controls

- only approved organization members can submit;
- bidder eligibility and NDA must be current;
- bid windows use server-authoritative times;
- bids are immutable after submission; revisions create new versions;
- late bids require documented seller approval and audit event;
- no bidder can view competing bids unless the process rules permit it;
- seller communications and bidder Q&A are preserved;
- securities orders are routed to the approved intermediary/venue rather than matched by Tourify.

## Negotiation record

Store structured term versions for price, asset scope, assumed liabilities, holdback, escrow, earn-out, recoupment treatment, representations, warranties, indemnities, closing conditions, transition services, approvals, and post-closing obligations.

## Auction fairness

The system must disclose process rules, seller discretion, reserve price treatment, affiliate participation, conflicts, and whether the seller may accept a non-highest bid. Tourify should not advertise an auction as producing “fair market value.”
