# Catalog Underwriting and Investment Committee Workflow

## Underwriting case

An underwriting case is versioned and tied to one eligibility and disclosure snapshot. It includes:

- proposed transaction and rights scope;
- revenue history and normalization quality;
- forecast scenarios;
- valuation range and methodology;
- concentration and durability metrics;
- rights-confidence and dispute adjustments;
- counterparty and operational risk;
- AI/provenance and fraud findings;
- downside, base, upside, and stress cases;
- required covenants, reserves, escrows, indemnities, insurance, or price adjustments;
- open conditions and decision recommendation.

## Investment committee controls

Tourify can provide an IC workflow but must not make the investment recommendation. Required controls include:

- institution-defined voting policy;
- conflict declarations;
- recusal support;
- quorum and approval thresholds;
- immutable materials delivered to voters;
- questions and management responses;
- vote records and conditions;
- decision expiration and refresh triggers.

## Model separation

- Phase 3 catalog valuation is an analytical estimate.
- Institutional underwriting is institution-specific.
- A fairness opinion, appraisal, NAV, audit conclusion, credit rating, or investment recommendation must come from the appropriately appointed provider.

## Change triggers

Require re-underwriting or IC refresh for material rights disputes, distributor changes, statement restatements, revenue deterioration, new liens, lost consents, major release events, fraud findings, or valuation-model changes.
