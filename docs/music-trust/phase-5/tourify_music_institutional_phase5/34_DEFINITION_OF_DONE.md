# Phase 5 Definition of Done

Phase 5 is complete only when all applicable items have evidence in the execution plan.

1. Phase 1–4 regression baseline passes before and after implementation.
2. Current repository, deployed schema, RLS, storage, and partner architecture are documented.
3. Direct asset, security, fund, and structured-finance pathways are explicitly separated.
4. Every opportunity has an approved, versioned transaction-classification record.
5. Institutional organizations use verified authority and role-limited access.
6. Eligibility assertions are provider-sourced, scoped, expiring, and revocable.
7. Catalog eligibility uses immutable Phase 2/3 snapshots and blocking rules.
8. Data rooms are private, versioned, hashed, logged, and access-revocable.
9. Frozen disclosure sets support IOIs, bids, IC decisions, subscriptions, and closings.
10. Diligence requests and findings are structured, attributable, and versioned.
11. Underwriting and investment-committee workflows preserve conflicts, votes, and conditions.
12. Tourify estimates are not mislabeled as NAV, appraisal, fairness opinion, rating, or recommendation.
13. IOI, bid, auction, and negotiation states are deterministic and audited.
14. Securities orders are handled by approved intermediaries/venues rather than a Tourify matcher.
15. Direct catalog transactions preserve legal-document and registration/noticing control.
16. Revenue cutovers use effective-dated snapshots without rewriting history.
17. Fund/SPV records identify every responsible adviser, administrator, custodian, bank, auditor, and intermediary.
18. Commitments, capital calls, subscriptions, and closings reconcile to official provider records.
19. Fund administrator remains source of truth for NAV and partnership accounting.
20. Finalized NAVs and statements are restated through new versions, never overwritten.
21. Waterfall calculations use deterministic integer/rational math and compare to official results.
22. Tourify never holds or transmits institutional cash or securities.
23. Portfolio risk snapshots are model-versioned and separate facts from estimates.
24. Stress scenarios, limits, and alerts do not execute discretionary transactions.
25. Benchmark methodology, data rights, bias controls, and limitations are documented.
26. Institutional secondaries enforce transfer restrictions, disclosures, conflicts, and official settlement.
27. Securitization, lending, leverage, and collateral features remain disabled without separate approvals.
28. Multi-venue data identifies the responsible broker and preserves execution-quality evidence.
29. Tokenized records reconcile to the approved official ownership model.
30. No permissionless, anonymous, bridged, or DeFi transfer path exists.
31. Cross-border access is allowlisted by approved jurisdiction modules.
32. Institutional APIs are scoped, versioned, rate limited, audited, and data-license aware.
33. New tables and storage pass RLS, tenant-isolation, and privacy tests.
34. Partner adapters pass signature, idempotency, retry, ordering, and reconciliation tests.
35. Admin actions enforce separation of duties and maker-checker approvals.
36. MNPI, clean-team, restricted-list, export, and data-use controls are tested.
37. Model changes, overrides, validation, and drift monitoring are documented.
38. Backup, restore, provider outage, and incident-response exercises pass.
39. Pilot transactions reconcile documents, rights, cash status, and official records end to end.
40. All user-facing workflows have feature flags, kill switches, rollback evidence, and accessible blocked/error states.
41. No critical security, legal-role, rights, financial, or reconciliation blocker remains.
42. `PHASE_5_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md` addresses every item above.
