# Credit, Collateral, and Lending Boundary

## Default position

Tourify does not lend, arrange loans, take collateral, hold security interests, calculate borrowing availability for execution, or liquidate music rights.

## Readiness-only capabilities

Phase 5 may support a diligence/readiness report for an approved lender covering:

- verified rights and ownership scope;
- historical cash flows and collection timing;
- liens, pledges, and encumbrances;
- recoupment and senior claims;
- concentration and volatility;
- valuation range and confidence;
- consent and assignment restrictions;
- monitoring data and covenant metrics.

## Partner boundary

The lender and counsel control underwriting, loan documents, perfection, UCC/copyright filings, collateral custody, borrowing base, covenants, defaults, remedies, and enforcement.

## Safety controls

- no one-click pledge of a catalog;
- no automatic collateral status based solely on a Tourify record;
- no wallet-based liquidation;
- no leverage for fan or retail products;
- no rehypothecation;
- no credit scoring presented as an offer or approval.
