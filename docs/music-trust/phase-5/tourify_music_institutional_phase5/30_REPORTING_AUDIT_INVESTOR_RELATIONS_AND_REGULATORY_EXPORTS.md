# Reporting, Audit, Investor Relations, and Regulatory Exports

## Institutional reports

- catalog and rights inventory;
- royalty-source and cash reconciliation;
- portfolio holdings and concentration;
- valuation and NAV support;
- actual versus forecast;
- distributions and capital activity;
- diligence and exception status;
- rights disputes and material events;
- transaction and closing history;
- partner reconciliation and control reports.

## Investor and LP reporting

Tourify may present reports approved by the fund administrator/adviser. Official statements, capital accounts, audited financials, tax documents, and notices must retain provider identity, version, issue date, and correction history.

## Audit support

Provide immutable exports of:

- source statements and hashes;
- rights and ownership snapshots;
- calculation traces;
- approval events;
- partner events and reconciliations;
- user access and data-room activity;
- model versions and overrides;
- transaction documents and closing evidence.

## Regulatory exports

Build configurable export infrastructure, not hardcoded legal filings. Current reporting such as Form ADV, Form PF, private-placement filings, tax reporting, or jurisdictional reports must be generated or filed by the responsible adviser, intermediary, administrator, tax provider, or counsel.

## Material events

Support an approved reporting calendar and notifications for restatements, rights disputes, provider changes, missed payments, breaches, valuation changes, litigation, and other defined material events.
