# Transaction Classification and Legal Pathways

## Why classification is mandatory

A direct transfer of copyright or royalty rights, a license, a sale of an entity, a note, a fund interest, a revenue-participation instrument, and an asset-backed security are not interchangeable. Phase 5 must not infer the legal pathway from product language such as “sell shares of my catalog.”

## Classification record

Every opportunity requires an immutable, counsel- or partner-approved classification record containing:

- proposed economic objective;
- underlying assets and rights scopes;
- seller/issuer legal entity;
- buyer/investor population;
- consideration and payment mechanics;
- duration, territory, exclusivity, control, and reversion;
- whether ownership, a license, a contractual payment right, debt, equity, or fund interest is created;
- offering/exemption and transfer restrictions where securities are involved;
- regulated parties responsible for solicitation, recommendation, execution, custody, recordkeeping, and settlement;
- approval date, approver, supporting memo reference, and expiration/re-review date.

## Supported planning pathways

### Direct asset sale or assignment

Potentially transfers identified copyright, master, composition, royalty, administration, or contractual rights. Requires chain-of-title, consent, notice, registration, tax, and closing analysis.

### Exclusive or non-exclusive license

Grants specified rights for a defined scope, territory, term, and use. Ownership may remain with the licensor.

### Entity or membership-interest transaction

Transfers interests in an entity that owns catalog rights. May be a securities transaction and requires separate corporate and regulatory analysis.

### Private security or revenue-participation instrument

Creates a financial instrument. Phase 4 partner, disclosure, eligibility, official-record, transfer, and surveillance controls remain mandatory.

### Private fund or SPV interest

Creates an interest in a pooled vehicle. Requires fund, adviser, offering, investor-eligibility, administration, custody, audit, tax, and reporting analysis.

### Securitized or structured-finance product

Pools receivables or rights into debt or asset-backed instruments. Disabled by default pending a dedicated legal, ratings, trustee, collateral, bankruptcy-remoteness, servicing, and disclosure workstream.

## Default-deny rules

The system must block bidding, subscriptions, token creation, closing, or transfers when:

- classification is missing, expired, or disputed;
- the responsible regulated party is not identified;
- the rights snapshot contains unresolved ownership disputes;
- seller authority or required consents are incomplete;
- the proposed product would cause Tourify to take custody, exercise investment discretion, or operate a trading venue;
- legal documents and product UI describe different investor or buyer rights.

Classification is a workflow gate, not legal advice generated by software.
