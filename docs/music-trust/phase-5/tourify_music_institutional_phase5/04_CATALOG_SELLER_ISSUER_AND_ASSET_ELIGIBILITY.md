# Catalog, Seller, Issuer, and Asset Eligibility

## Eligibility dimensions

A catalog-capital opportunity is eligible only when all required dimensions pass:

- identity and authority;
- chain of title and rights scope;
- agreements and required consents;
- Rights Passport status;
- registration and identifier quality;
- royalty-source provenance and reconciliation;
- dispute, lien, recoupment, reversion, and exclusivity status;
- tax and entity readiness;
- historical revenue sufficiency;
- fraud and manipulation screening;
- technical asset availability;
- legal pathway and partner approval.

## Catalog asset graph

Eligibility must operate on a graph, not a flat track list:

```text
Seller / Issuer
├── Legal entity
├── Musical works
├── Sound recordings
├── Releases and ISRC/ISWC/UPC relationships
├── Rights and administration scopes
├── Agreements and amendments
├── Revenue sources and statements
├── Recoupment and encumbrances
└── Disputes, claims, notices, and restrictions
```

## Snapshot requirement

Opening a data room creates an immutable eligibility snapshot referencing exact versions of:

- Rights Passports;
- claims and agreements;
- registration records;
- royalty reconciliation periods;
- valuation model and assumptions;
- disputes and exceptions;
- seller representations.

Later changes create a new snapshot and a material-change alert. Existing bidders retain access to the exact data on which their prior IOI or bid was based.

## Blocking conditions

Examples include:

- unresolved master or composition ownership dispute;
- unverified seller authority;
- material missing statements or unexplained revenue gaps;
- rights already assigned or pledged inconsistently with the proposal;
- samples or derivative rights lacking required clearance;
- inconsistent catalog identifiers affecting revenue matching;
- inability to define the asset or revenue stream being transferred;
- unapproved use of artist name, likeness, or promotional obligations.

An ineligible catalog can remain on Tourify and continue normal playback. Only the institutional transaction feature is blocked.
