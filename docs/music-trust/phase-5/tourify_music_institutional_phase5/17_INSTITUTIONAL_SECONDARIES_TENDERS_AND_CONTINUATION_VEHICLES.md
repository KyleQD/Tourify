# Institutional Secondaries, Tenders, and Continuation Vehicles

## Supported partner-led workflows

- transfer of fund/SPV interests;
- issuer or sponsor tender offers;
- LP-led secondary sale;
- adviser-led secondary or continuation transaction;
- direct catalog resale;
- portfolio strip or partial-interest transaction.

## Required controls

- legal pathway and transfer restrictions;
- official recordkeeper and venue/intermediary;
- seller and buyer eligibility;
- current disclosures and financial information;
- conflicts and related-party identification;
- valuation/fairness materials where required by current law, documents, or policy;
- consent and right-of-first-refusal workflows;
- bid and allocation rules;
- settlement and position reconciliation;
- post-transfer tax and reporting updates.

## Continuation vehicles

Continuation transactions require separate vehicle, adviser, conflict, valuation, election, rollover, liquidity-option, and expense-allocation analysis. Tourify can manage evidence and elections but not make the investor’s choice or provide investment advice.

## Data segregation

Potential buyers receive only authorized data. Existing investors’ confidential information and side-letter terms remain restricted.
