## Artist integration

Add an optional **Catalog Capital** area under the existing artist music workspace:

- readiness summary;
- eligible catalog scope;
- missing rights/revenue evidence;
- request institutional review;
- active mandate/deal status;
- data-room permissions;
- bids and closing status;
- post-closing reporting.

Do not add institutional prompts to ordinary listener playback or require sellers to change distributors.

## Institutional workspace

Use existing Tourify design primitives and provide:

- role-aware dashboards;
- clear source-of-truth labels;
- confidence and freshness indicators;
- immutable snapshot/version display;
- visible conflicts and unresolved exceptions;
- accessible tables and export controls;
- designed loading, empty, blocked, stale, and reconciliation states.

## Financial language

Never display “guaranteed,” “safe,” “approved investment,” “fair value,” “liquid,” or “AI protected” unless the exact approved source and scope support it. Separate asking price, analytical range, official NAV, appraisal, and executed price visually.

## Mobile

Institutional transaction and data-room actions can remain web-only initially. Mobile may provide read-only alerts and status after security review.
