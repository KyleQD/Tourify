# Internal and External Oversight, Audit, Evaluation and Investigations

## Purpose

Establish independent accountability functions before privileges or large public funding are considered.

## Non-negotiable boundary

- Preserve `artist_music` and every existing upload, storage, signed-stream, access-control, Jukebox, mobile, feed, profile, EPK, marketplace, analytics, licensing, royalty, administration, federation, commons, constitutional, and convention path.
- Use additive migrations, explicit backfills, RLS, restricted storage, short-lived signed URLs, append-only audit/outbox events, feature flags, and compensating actions. Never reset the database.
- Tourify remains an optional technical provider. No institution may depend on Tourify remaining available.
- A private convention cannot grant treaty capacity, privileges, immunities, diplomatic status, legal personality under international law, or public regulatory power.
- No state, international organization, public authority, creator, cooperative, or local organization becomes a member by inference.
- Identifiers and credentials are references and signed statements; they do not create copyright ownership or licensing, administration, collection, payment, enforcement, diplomatic, or representation authority.
- Local creator and organization sovereignty, withdrawal, and provider portability remain protected.
- Public projections expose only approved minimal data and are never the sole authorization source for a high-impact action.
- Every durable action records policy, schema, jurisdiction, effective period, authoritative source manifest, actor authority, idempotency key, and audit event.


## Required outcomes

The model separates management, internal audit, external audit, evaluation, ethics, investigations, ombuds, and governing-body oversight. Appointment, access, independence, budget, reporting, publication, recommendation tracking, retaliation protection, and conflicts are documented.

## Source-of-truth rules

The current constitutive instrument, participant authority, membership status, governing-body decision, host agreement, privilege schedule, budget appropriation, authoritative rights source, and jurisdiction module control the action. Historical records are never reinterpreted silently after a policy or schema change.

## State and evidence model

Every state transition is explicit, actor-scoped, evidence-backed, idempotent, time-bounded where applicable, appealable, and reversible through a documented compensating action. Draft, proposed, approved, effective, suspended, revoked, withdrawn, expired, superseded, and rejected remain distinct states.

## Stop conditions

Stop when legal character, competent authority, constitutive basis, membership, source lineage, host arrangement, jurisdiction, funding, privacy, security, accessibility, competition, procurement, staffing, provider contract, independent review, or public approval is missing, disputed, expired, or stale.

## Implementation evidence

Codex records exact files changed, deployed database objects, policy and schema versions, migrations, generated types, RLS tests, route and worker tests, external assumptions, reviews, monitoring, owners, rollback instructions, commands, results, and unresolved blockers in the Phase 15 execution plan.
