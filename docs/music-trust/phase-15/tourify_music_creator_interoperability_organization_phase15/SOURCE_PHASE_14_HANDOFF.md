# Source Phase 14 Handoff

Phase 15 consumes the Phase 14 Global Creator Interoperability Convention handoff and requires multiple years of safe voluntary interoperability before any public-law institution is considered.

The Phase 14 convention remains a private compact unless competent states or international organizations establish a legally valid constitutive instrument. Phase 15 cannot inherit treaty status, public powers, privileges, immunities, diplomatic capacity, representation authority, or state participation from Phase 14 records or feature flags.
