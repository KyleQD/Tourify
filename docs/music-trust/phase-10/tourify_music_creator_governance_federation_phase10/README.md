# Tourify Global Creator Governance Federation Readiness — Phase 10

Phase 10 extends Tourify's Phase 9 creator-data cooperative readiness into a controlled architecture for federated creator-governed organizations, interoperable credentials, cross-border data institutions, sovereign governance and separately approved representation networks.

## Launch boundary

Tourify remains the platform and technical service provider. The federation and every member organization require separate legal existence, membership decisions, governing documents, finances, authority, credentials and operating controls. A Tourify account, upload, Rights Passport, Phase 8 consent or Phase 9 cooperative membership does not create federation membership, data-transfer authority, voting delegation, representation, collective licensing or bargaining authority.

## First releasable product

The first releasable product is limited to:

1. federation-readiness education;
2. organization applications and diligence;
3. reserved-power and local-sovereignty configuration;
4. sandbox credential issuance and verification;
5. scoped mandates for non-representational administrative services;
6. bilateral service discovery; and
7. cross-border transfer assessment records without automatic data pooling.

Global representation, collective licensing, bargaining, coordinated pricing, public data pooling, tokenized membership and production cross-border research remain disabled.
