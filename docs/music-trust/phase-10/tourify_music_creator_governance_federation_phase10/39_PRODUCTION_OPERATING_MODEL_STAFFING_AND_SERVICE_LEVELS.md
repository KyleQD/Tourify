# Production Operating Model, Staffing and Service Levels

## Operating roles

- Federation secretary or administrator.
- Member-organization relationship lead.
- Governance operations manager.
- Credential and trust-registry operator.
- Privacy and cross-border transfer reviewer.
- Security and key-management owner.
- Standards and conformance lead.
- Competition and collective-action reviewer.
- Incident commander.
- Independent appeals and audit providers.

## Service levels

Every service has a named owner, coverage hours, severity model, response target, review deadline, escalation path, external dependency, monitoring dashboard and rollback procedure.

High-risk services include:

- issuer accreditation and suspension;
- credential status and key compromise;
- mandate activation and revocation propagation;
- cross-border data transfer approval;
- governance voting and ratification;
- public trust-registry publication;
- regulator requests and legal holds;
- federation partition and continuity.

## Launch staffing gate

No production launch occurs until the responsible roles are staffed or contracted, on-call and incident procedures are exercised, independent review is funded, and at least two trained people can perform every critical control without sharing credentials.
