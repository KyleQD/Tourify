# Federation Capture, Power Concentration and Member Protection

## Purpose

Prevent Tourify, large member organizations, funders, technology providers or a geographic bloc from capturing federation governance or using technical control to defeat democratic decisions.

## Required controls

- Published representative-allocation and voting rules.
- Concentration limits and independent-controller tests.
- Conflict disclosure, recusal and related-party review.
- Separate root-key, trust-registry, finance and audit authorities.
- Member inspection, complaint and whistleblower channels.
- Non-retaliation across Tourify discovery, licensing, enforcement, payments and support.
- Funding restrictions that preserve member control.
- Emergency powers that expire automatically.
- Local exit and data-portability rights.
- Independent periodic governance review.

## Stop conditions

- One organization or related group can unilaterally reach quorum or amendment threshold.
- Tourify can change federation rules through platform configuration alone.
- A funder receives governance rights inconsistent with governing documents.
- Dissenting organizations face service degradation or commercial retaliation.
- The trust registry, credential root and audit system share one uncontrolled administrator.

## Required tests

- Related-party and beneficial-control aggregation.
- Quorum and concentration simulations.
- Recusal and substitute-review flow.
- Emergency-power expiry.
- Organization exit during a disputed federation decision.
- Non-retaliation regression across existing Tourify surfaces.
