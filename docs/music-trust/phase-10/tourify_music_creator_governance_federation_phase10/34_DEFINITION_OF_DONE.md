# Phase 10 Definition of Done

Phase 10 is not complete because pages render or credentials can be issued. It is complete only when federation readiness is evidenced across legal, governance, technical, privacy, security and operating dimensions.

## Mandatory completion gates

1. **Audit complete** — actual repository paths, deployed database objects, migration head, provider contracts, roles, flags and operational owners are recorded.
2. **Entity separation proven** — Tourify, the federation and every member organization have distinct records, agreements, authority and financial boundaries.
3. **Sovereignty enforced** — reserved powers, local ratification and default-deny delegation are implemented and tested.
4. **Credential lifecycle complete** — issuance, presentation, verification, selective disclosure, expiry, suspension, revocation, replacement, key rotation and recovery are tested.
5. **Mandates exact-scoped** — no action executes without current right, service, territory, term, counterparty and subdelegation authority.
6. **Cross-border controls proven** — transfer mechanism, localization, onward transfer, consent, retention and emergency suspension are versioned and tested.
7. **Governance recoverable** — proposals, ballots, quorum, veto, ratification, conflicts, appeals and compensating actions are auditable.
8. **No implied collective authority** — representation, collective licensing, bargaining, coordinated pricing and tokenized membership remain disabled absent a separate approval package.
9. **Security reviewed** — keys, trust registry, privileged access, incident response, federation partition and continuity pass independent review.
10. **Existing system preserved** — upload, private storage, signed streaming, access, Jukebox, mobile, marketplace, feed, profile, EPK, analytics, Rights Passport, royalty, licensing and rights-administration regressions remain green.

## Evidence required per task

- files changed;
- migrations and generated types;
- commands run;
- unit, integration, route, RLS, worker and regression results;
- screenshots or API payloads where applicable;
- legal/governance/privacy/security review references;
- feature flags and rollout state;
- monitoring and rollback instructions;
- unresolved blockers and named owner.

A task without evidence remains incomplete.
