# Source Phase 9 Handoff — Phase 10 Global Creator Governance Federation Readiness

## Purpose

Define a future handoff for federated creator-governed organizations, interoperable member credentials, cross-border data institutions and approved representation networks.

## Primary workflows

1. Prove Phase 9 entity independence, member governance, research ethics, benefit allocation and cross-border controls.
2. Define federation membership and sovereignty.
3. Create interoperable but revocable credentials and mandates.
4. Prevent one entity from overriding another’s member decisions.
5. Obtain regulator and standards engagement before production.

## Required controls

- Phase 10 cannot launch from Phase 9 feature flags.
- No global representation mandate by inference.
- No cross-entity data pooling without new contribution and transfer approval.

## Primary records

- `future_phase10_approval_packages`

## Canonical source-of-truth rules

- `artist_music` remains the catalog anchor; Phase 9 stores references and approved extracts, not replacement catalog rows.
- Rights Passports, Phase 6 licences and Phase 7 administration/enforcement records remain immutable source evidence.
- Phase 8 consent, privacy and benchmark decisions are inputs, but cooperative membership and data contribution require new, separately executed records.
- External research institutions, regulators, standards bodies, CMOs, courts and government registries remain authoritative for their own decisions and filings.
- Every derived dataset, analysis, publication and benefit allocation points to an immutable input manifest and policy version.

## Stop conditions

Stop the workflow when membership, consent, contribution licence, authority, source lineage, privacy, ethics, competition, security, jurisdiction or required approval is missing; when a cohort fails privacy controls; when competitively sensitive information may be exposed; or when an output could be understood as coordinated pricing, legal advice or representation without authority.

## Existing-system integration

Consume approved, versioned extracts through adapters or event outboxes. Never query confidential operational tables directly from public or researcher-facing routes. Do not modify upload, stream, entitlement, player, feed, profile, EPK, marketplace, licensing, rights-administration, royalty or mobile behavior.

## Required tests

Membership separation, purpose-specific consent, RLS, small-cohort and re-identification controls, default-deny access, competition-sensitive content escalation, idempotent external actions, compensating rollback and existing music regressions.

## Completion evidence

Codex records audited paths and deployed objects, legal/entity assumptions, migrations and generated types, RLS and route tests, privacy/ethics/competition/security reviews, feature flags, stop conditions, monitoring, rollback and task-level evidence in the Phase 10 execution plan.
