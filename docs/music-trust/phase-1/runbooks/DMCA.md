# Music Trust DMCA Runbook

This operational note does not replace legal counsel or the platform's formal DMCA policy.

1. Forward a facially complete copyright notice to the designated DMCA channel and preserve receipt time and original submission.
2. Validate required notice elements under the current legal policy. Do not treat a trust label, origin hash, or certificate as dispositive.
3. Apply access restrictions directed by the authorized DMCA operator; preserve private records and append report events.
4. Notify the affected user using approved notice text and counter-notice instructions.
5. Process counter-notices and restoration timing only under counsel-approved procedures.
6. Record final disposition, actor, timestamp, and related case/report IDs. Keep evidence access server-mediated.
