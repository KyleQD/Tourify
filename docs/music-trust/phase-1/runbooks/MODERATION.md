# Music Trust Moderation Runbook

1. Preserve the report and append a `content_report_events` entry for every status change.
2. Restrict the track when credible harm is ongoing; do not delete origin, declaration, evidence, review, or certificate history.
3. Route ownership and copyright allegations to the DMCA process when they meet its scope. Route likeness/impersonation and disclosure issues to platform moderation.
4. A fingerprint match is a private review signal, not proof of infringement or ownership.
5. Certification suspension is reversible while facts are reviewed. Revocation requires a structured reason and reviewer permission.
6. Notify the artist using artist-visible language that excludes reporter identity, private evidence, detection scores, and internal notes.
