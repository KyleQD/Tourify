# Product Scope and Account Tiers

## Core promise

> Upload your music. Build your catalog. Certify your work when you are ready.

Basic music upload remains a useful product. Certification is an optional evidence-based service, not a fee required to be treated as a human artist.

## Status vocabulary

Use exact, narrow labels:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Artist submitted | The uploader supplied the file and declarations. |
| Origin recorded | Tourify stored an integrity record and timestamp. |
| Human-created certified | The track passed a specific version of Tourify's review standard. |
| Rights Passport issued | Contributor and rights documentation reached the required completion level. |
| Copyright registration linked | A government registration reference was linked; Tourify did not issue it. |

Never use `copyright certified`, `ownership guaranteed`, or `100% AI-free`.

## Account packaging

### Artist — Free

- Limited private/profile uploads.
- External DSP links.
- Private source file in `artist-music`.
- Rights declaration and AI disclosure.
- SHA-256 integrity processing where worker capacity permits.
- Artist-submitted status.
- No certification badge.

### Artist+

- Higher catalog limits.
- Catalog import support.
- Origin-record credits.
- Certification discounts.
- Contributor invitations when Rights Passport is enabled.
- Advanced upload and catalog analytics.

### Artist Pro

- Larger catalog and archive allowance.
- Included certification credits.
- Priority certification review.
- Catalog discrepancy reporting.
- Rights Passport workspace when launched.
- Protection and monitoring discounts.

### À-la-carte services

- Origin Record.
- Human-Created Certification.
- Rights Passport review.
- Catalog import/reconciliation.
- Protection/monitoring.

Pricing must remain configuration-driven and outside the core trust-state machine.

## Human-only product policy

Certification is optional. Compliance is not.

Every public upload requires:

- a rights declaration
- an AI-use disclosure
- policy acceptance
- baseline automated checks
- enforcement and appeal coverage

Materially AI-generated music is not eligible for human-created discovery, editorial, licensing, certification, or future rights-financing surfaces. Tourify may reject it from public hosting under the Human Music Policy.
