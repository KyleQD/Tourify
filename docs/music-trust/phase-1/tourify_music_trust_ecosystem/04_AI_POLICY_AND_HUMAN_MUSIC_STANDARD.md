# AI Policy and Human Music Standard

## Policy objective

Tourify is a platform for human artists. The policy must distinguish assistive production tools from systems that originate material expressive content.

## Human-created

Generally compatible with the human-created category:

- recording human performances
- human-written lyrics, melodies, harmony, rhythm, and arrangement
- conventional synthesizers and samplers controlled by a person
- licensed samples and loops with disclosure where required
- EQ, compression, limiting, editing, pitch correction, timing correction
- noise reduction and restoration
- non-generative mastering assistance

## Assistive AI

Allowed with disclosure and certification review:

- AI mastering
- stem separation
- denoising or restoration
- mix recommendations
- source repair that does not originate material musical expression

## Materially generated

Not eligible for the Human-Created catalog or certification:

- text-to-music output forming material parts of the track
- generated melodies, lyrics, arrangements, or performances represented as human-created
- synthetic singers represented as human performers
- unauthorized cloned voices or likenesses
- generated stems making up a material part of the release

## Upload declaration

Require the uploader to state:

- AI category
- tools used, if any
- what the tools did
- whether a voice or likeness was synthesized
- whether all contributors made compatible disclosures
- whether source files and sessions are available

## Enforcement

- Automated detection creates a private risk signal only.
- No model may make an irreversible infringement or fraud decision.
- High-risk submissions enter human review.
- The artist can respond and appeal.
- Proven intentional misrepresentation can suspend certification and restrict the account.
- A non-certified track is not automatically suspected of AI use.

## Versioning

The standard and declaration text must have stable versions. Every upload declaration records the exact versions accepted by the artist.
