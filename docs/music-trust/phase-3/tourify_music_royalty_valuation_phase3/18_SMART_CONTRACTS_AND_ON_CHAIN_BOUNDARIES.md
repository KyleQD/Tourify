# Smart Contracts and On-Chain Boundaries

## Phase 3 contract options

1. Continue the Phase 2 attestation registry for valuation and distribution-report commitments.
2. Add a nonfinancial utility collectible contract after policy review.
3. For regulated instruments, deploy only with the regulated partner and specialist smart-contract counsel.

## Never store on-chain

- raw royalty statements;
- personal or tax data;
- private contracts;
- bank details;
- full revenue amounts when confidentiality applies;
- unrestricted wallet-to-identity mappings;
- mutable legal ownership as an unqualified public fact.

## Security controls

- minimal contracts;
- established audited libraries;
- role separation and multisig;
- emergency pause where appropriate;
- explicit upgrade or immutable-version policy;
- chain and contract allowlist;
- source verification;
- independent audit before mainnet;
- testnet pilot;
- replay and duplicate protection;
- event reconciliation;
- key rotation and compromise runbook.

## No autonomous payout contract initially

External royalties arrive through off-chain payors, include corrections and disputes, and require tax/compliance controls. Keep payout calculation and authorization in the audited financial service; on-chain commitments may prove a distribution statement without moving funds.
