# Catalog Valuation Methodology

## Product definition

Tourify Catalog Value is a model-generated analytical range for a defined rights interest as of a defined date. It is not a guaranteed sale price, accounting fair value, appraisal, tax valuation, fairness opinion, investment recommendation, or token market price.

## Eligibility

Require:

- issued, active Rights Passport;
- defined valued interest and ownership percentage;
- sufficient accepted revenue history;
- resolved material disputes or explicit exclusion;
- known contract term, territory, recoupment, and restrictions;
- source quality above a minimum threshold;
- model-supported asset and revenue categories.

## Approaches

### Income approach

Forecast net cash flows and discount them for time and risk. Use monthly or quarterly cohorts, decay curves, growth assumptions, rights term, recoupment, fees, withholding, concentration, and scenario-specific discount rates.

### Market approach

Use curated comparable transactions only when rights scope, term, revenue quality, genre, age, concentration, and buyer conditions are sufficiently comparable. Do not present opaque revenue multiples as universal truth.

### Cost approach

Generally weak for established catalog earning power, but may inform replacement or production cost for limited internal uses.

## Output

- downside, base, and upside ranges;
- expected net present value;
- implied multiple as a derived metric;
- confidence score;
- major sensitivities;
- excluded revenue;
- valuation date and model version;
- whether an independent reviewer participated.
