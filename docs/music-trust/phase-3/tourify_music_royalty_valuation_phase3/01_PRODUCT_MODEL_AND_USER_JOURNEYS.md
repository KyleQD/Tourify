# Product Model and User Journeys

## Artist journey: connect earnings

1. Artist opens `/artist/music/earnings`.
2. Tourify explains which revenue sources it can import and which rights each source represents.
3. Artist uploads a statement or authorizes a provider connector.
4. Import remains `processing` until the file is validated, hashed, parsed, normalized, matched, and reviewed.
5. Artist sees matched, unmatched, duplicate, conflicting, and excluded lines.
6. Artist resolves unmatched identifiers or requests a Rights Operations review.
7. Only accepted batches post to the royalty ledger.

## Collaborator journey: transparent allocation

1. A participant receives a notification that earnings were allocated to an accepted Rights Passport interest.
2. They can inspect the source statement, usage period, gross amount, deductions, recoupment, allocated share, holds, and payable balance subject to their permissions.
3. They complete payout onboarding with the selected payment provider.
4. They receive a downloadable statement and payment status history.
5. They may dispute a line without altering the original source data.

## Artist journey: valuation

1. Artist selects an eligible catalog or release.
2. Tourify checks minimum verified revenue history and rights confidence.
3. The valuation engine creates base, downside, and upside scenarios.
4. The UI shows a range, valuation date, model version, inputs, exclusions, confidence, and major risks.
5. The result is labeled an analytical estimate, not an appraisal, guaranteed sale price, investment recommendation, or token price.
6. Artists can request an independent valuation review for financing use.

## Fan journey: utility only

Fans may buy or earn access collectibles tied to tangible benefits such as presales, private streams, community access, merchandise, or experiences. Utility items must be usable at issuance, sold in quantities consistent with likely use, and not marketed around appreciation, resale, revenue share, or Tourify efforts increasing value.

## Regulated financing journey

1. Artist completes rights, revenue, identity, tax, and diligence gates.
2. Tourify creates a financing-readiness data room.
3. A regulated partner determines structure, eligibility, disclosures, investor onboarding, offering exemption, custody, transfer restrictions, and settlement.
4. Tourify may supply technology and verified data but does not accept investment orders unless counsel and partner contracts expressly allow it.
5. Any token mirrors a legally controlling instrument and transfer-agent or partner ledger.
