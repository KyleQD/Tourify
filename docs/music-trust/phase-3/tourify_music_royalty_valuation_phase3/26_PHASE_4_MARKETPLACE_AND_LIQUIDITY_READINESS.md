# Phase 4 Marketplace and Liquidity Readiness

Phase 4 may evaluate regulated primary offerings and controlled secondary liquidity. Phase 3 must provide stable interfaces without assuming Phase 4 will be approved.

## Required outputs

- reconciled revenue snapshots;
- participant and offering distribution statements;
- transfer eligibility read model;
- partner position reconciliation;
- instrument maturity and termination events;
- investor communication archive;
- immutable disclosure versions;
- tax-lot and cost-basis data supplied by the responsible party;
- complete compliance holds and sanctions status.

## Phase 4 decisions

- partner versus Tourify regulatory roles;
- funding portal, broker-dealer, ATS, transfer agent, custodian, and qualified-custodian requirements;
- state securities laws;
- investor limits and suitability;
- market surveillance;
- best execution and conflicts;
- digital-asset broker reporting;
- liquidity and valuation disclosures.
