# Current Standards, Legal, Tax, and Regulatory Research

This document is a research baseline, not legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice. Codex must not turn it into product claims. Counsel must confirm the current rules at each launch gate.

## Royalty data

DDEX DSR has multiple current flat-file profiles. Part 3 covers basic audio usage, Part 6 communicates actual royalty data in direct licensor relationships, Part 9 supports financial reporting to record companies/distributors, and Part 11 supports reporting to The MLC. DDEX advises using the latest profile version and requires an Implementation Licence before live implementation.

## Royalty accounting

The MLC receives monthly usage data and royalties from DSPs operating under the U.S. blanket mechanical license and pays eligible rights holders. The Copyright Office publishes audit-related rules and resources emphasizing reproducible royalty calculations and auditability. SoundExchange and PRO/CMO income remain separate source classes.

## Tokenized instruments

On March 17, 2026, the SEC issued an interpretation on federal securities laws and crypto assets. On January 28, 2026, SEC divisions published a statement describing tokenized securities as securities represented through crypto networks. A token does not remove the financial instrument from securities requirements.

## Offering paths

SEC resources describe Regulation Crowdfunding, Regulation D, Regulation A, Rule 504, intrastate, and registered offerings. Reg CF transactions must occur through an SEC-registered broker-dealer or funding portal and have issuer and investor requirements.

## Money transmission and sanctions

FinCEN guidance states that administrators or exchangers accepting and transmitting convertible virtual currency may be money transmitters depending on facts and exemptions. OFAC obligations apply to digital and traditional currency and require risk-based screening.

## Tax

IRS 2026 instructions require digital-asset brokers to use Form 1099-DA for covered broker transactions after 2025. Royalty payments of qualifying amounts may require Form 1099-MISC. Product roles and transaction facts determine reporting; use specialist tax counsel.

## Valuation

The current IVS edition effective January 31, 2025 includes IVS 210 for intangible assets and requires use of applicable general and asset standards. Tourify's internal analytical estimate should not be labeled IVS-compliant unless performed under the full standard by qualified personnel and appropriate scope.
