# Regulated Royalty Participation Pilot

A contractual right to future royalties offered to purchasers for profit is likely to require securities analysis even if represented by a token. The 2026 SEC interpretation and tokenized-securities statement should be reviewed by specialized counsel for the exact structure.

## Partner-led model

Tourify should initially operate as data and workflow infrastructure. A regulated partner should control, as applicable:

- issuer structuring;
- securities-law exemption or registration;
- investor eligibility and limits;
- offering disclosures;
- broker-dealer or funding-portal activity;
- order handling;
- escrow and settlement;
- transfer-agent/ownership records;
- custody;
- AML/KYC and sanctions;
- tax reporting;
- secondary-transfer restrictions.

## Candidate offering paths for counsel review

- Regulation Crowdfunding through a registered intermediary;
- Regulation D private placement;
- Regulation A qualified offering;
- nonsecurity loan or advance structure with a licensed lender/finance partner;
- label/distributor advance without fan investment.

The plan must not select a pathway without issuer-specific counsel.

## Readiness gates

- active Rights Passport and no material unresolved dispute;
- at least 12–24 months of reconciled revenue unless partner approves otherwise;
- verified payor and chain of title;
- complete contracts and recoupment data;
- independent financial review;
- valuation governance approval;
- issuer entity and tax analysis;
- offering data room;
- written partner responsibilities matrix.
