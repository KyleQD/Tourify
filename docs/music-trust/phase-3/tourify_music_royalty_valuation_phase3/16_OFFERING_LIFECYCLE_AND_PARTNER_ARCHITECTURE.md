# Offering Lifecycle and Partner Architecture

## Lifecycle

`candidate → diligence → structured → filed/qualified/exempt → open → funded → active reporting → distributions → matured/terminated`

## Tourify responsibilities may include

- artist-facing application;
- rights and revenue data room;
- catalog analytics;
- disclosure-draft data feeds;
- status UX;
- partner API integration;
- post-close revenue reporting;
- benefit/community features.

## Partner responsibilities must be explicit

- determining security status;
- investor onboarding and suitability/limits;
- accepting orders and funds;
- escrow;
- issuance ledger;
- investor statements;
- regulatory filings;
- transfer restrictions;
- complaints and required records;
- distributions and withholding;
- broker and digital-asset tax reporting where applicable.

## Fail-closed integration

Tourify cannot show “Invest,” accept an order, reserve units, or transmit consideration until the partner returns an active-offering capability for that user, jurisdiction, and instrument.
