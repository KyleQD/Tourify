# Admin, Financial Operations, and Compliance

## Operations areas

- import exceptions;
- parser failures;
- identifier conflicts;
- unmatched and suspense balances;
- allocation approval;
- period close;
- payout failures and returned funds;
- payee verification requirements;
- sanctions/compliance alerts;
- tax-form readiness;
- valuation review and overrides;
- regulated-partner reconciliation;
- complaints and disputes.

## Segregation of duties

Separate capabilities for ingestion review, match approval, journal posting, allocation approval, payout approval, valuation approval, compliance hold, and system administration. High-risk actions require maker-checker approval.

## No silent edits

Staff may not overwrite source statements, posted journals, allocation outputs, valuation snapshots, or provider events. Corrections require versioning, reversal, reason, actor, and evidence.
