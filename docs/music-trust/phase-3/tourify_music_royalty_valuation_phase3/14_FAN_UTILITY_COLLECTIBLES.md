# Fan Utility Collectibles

## Purpose

Fan utility can launch before regulated royalty participation if it is designed for immediate consumptive use and does not promise profits, revenue, appreciation, or a Tourify-supported resale market.

## Possible benefits

- presale or waitlist access;
- private listening rooms;
- release commentary and stems where licensed;
- merchandise redemption;
- fan badges;
- event check-in benefits;
- community access;
- limited voting on nonfinancial creative choices;
- collectible artwork or credits.

## Controls

- benefits available at issuance;
- plain-language terms and expiration;
- quantities aligned with plausible use;
- no royalty share;
- no guaranteed scarcity value;
- transfer restrictions or non-transferability where appropriate;
- no marketing around investment returns;
- refund and failed-benefit policy;
- consumer-protection review;
- separation from Rights Passport verification badges.

## Technical options

Start with database-backed entitlements. Blockchain minting is optional and should be invisible to users unless it adds portability. Use embedded wallets only after privacy, account recovery, sanctions, fees, and custody implications are approved.
