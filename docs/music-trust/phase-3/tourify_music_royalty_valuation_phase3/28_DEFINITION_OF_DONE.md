# Phase 3 Definition of Done

Phase 3 is complete only when all applicable launch-scope items have recorded evidence:

1. Repository and database audit completed.
2. Phase 1/2 regression baseline captured.
3. Statement source files are private, hashed, versioned, and idempotent.
4. At least the approved pilot formats normalize into one canonical model.
5. Source totals reconcile to normalized totals.
6. Matching never relies only on title and artist.
7. Posted journals are balanced and immutable.
8. Corrections use reversals and replacements.
9. Allocations use the correct issued historical rights snapshot.
10. Territory, date, category, gross/net basis, deductions, and recoupment are enforced.
11. Unknown and disputed amounts route to suspense or holds.
12. Participants receive reproducible statements.
13. Payee onboarding avoids storing raw bank data in Tourify.
14. Payout webhooks are verified and idempotent.
15. Real payouts have maker-checker approval and reconciliation.
16. Tax and sanctions responsibilities are documented.
17. Valuations are versioned, reproducible ranges with confidence and assumptions.
18. Valuation changes do not affect rights or ledger balances.
19. Fan utility makes no profit or appreciation promise.
20. Regulated financing is partner-gated and cannot accept orders through unapproved Tourify paths.
21. No open secondary market exists.
22. Smart contracts contain no private source data and are independently reviewed before production use.
23. RLS and authorization tests cover artists, participants, teams, operations, compliance, and public users.
24. Security, backup, incident, payout-freeze, and key-compromise runbooks pass exercises.
25. Pilot calculations reconcile to expected results.
26. Existing upload, playback, marketplace, library, feed, EPK, and mobile tests pass.
27. Rollback and feature-disable procedures are verified.
28. All execution-plan tasks are complete, blocked with owner, or explicitly removed through approved scope change.
