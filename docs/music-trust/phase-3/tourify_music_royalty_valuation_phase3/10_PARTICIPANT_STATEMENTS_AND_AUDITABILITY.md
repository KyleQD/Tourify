# Participant Statements and Auditability

Participant statements must be understandable enough for a recipient to reproduce the calculation.

## Statement sections

- payee and period;
- beginning payable and recoupment balances;
- source/provider summary;
- asset and rights-category detail;
- units and territories;
- gross royalties;
- deductions and fees;
- contractual share;
- recoupment applied;
- reserves, disputes, and withholding;
- adjustments and reversals;
- net earned, paid, and ending payable;
- Rights Passport snapshot ID;
- allocation-run and model versions;
- dispute window and contact path.

## Export formats

Provide human-readable PDF/HTML, machine-readable CSV/JSON, and enterprise exports after requirements are confirmed. Every export includes a checksum and generation timestamp.

## Audit package

Authorized users can generate a package containing source references, normalized lines, match decisions, journals, allocation traces, agreements, rights snapshots, payout events, and change history. Sensitive data must be permission-filtered.
