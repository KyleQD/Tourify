# Security, Financial Controls, and Audit

## Security controls

- threat model for financial and rights fraud;
- encryption at rest and in transit;
- private signed URLs;
- least-privilege service credentials;
- secrets in managed server storage;
- connector token encryption and rotation;
- step-up authentication for payout and banking changes;
- audit logging for every money-affecting action;
- anomaly detection for duplicate statements, account takeover, payout redirection, and manipulation;
- backup and restoration tests;
- incident-response and payment-freeze runbooks.

## Financial controls

- balanced journal invariant;
- immutable close periods;
- approval thresholds;
- maker-checker payouts;
- daily provider reconciliation;
- aged suspense and failed-payout reporting;
- reserve and hold review;
- independent sampling of allocation calculations;
- model validation and change management;
- retained evidence sufficient for audit.

## Compliance review

Before any digital-asset or investment functionality, document securities, broker-dealer, investment-adviser, money-transmission, sanctions, privacy, consumer-protection, tax, custody, and state-law analyses.
