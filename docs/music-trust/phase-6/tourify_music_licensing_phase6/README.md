# Tourify Global Licensing and Clearance Exchange — Phase 6

Phase 6 extends the safe upload, Rights Passport, royalty/valuation, regulated marketplace and institutional systems into a controlled global music licensing and clearance exchange.

## Launch boundary

Tourify is the verified catalog, rights/authority data, workflow, evidence, collaboration, contract, delivery, usage and reporting layer. The executed legal agreement and approved rights holders/representatives grant each licence. CMOs, publishers, labels, payment providers, tax professionals, insurers and counsel retain their assigned roles.
