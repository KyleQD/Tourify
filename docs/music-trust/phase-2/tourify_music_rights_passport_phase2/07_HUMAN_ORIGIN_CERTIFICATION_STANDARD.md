# Human-Origin Certification Standard

## Purpose

Define what Tourify means by Human-Created Certified and prevent marketing claims from exceeding the evidence.

## Classifications

### Human-Created Certified

Human authors determined the expressive musical content. Conventional editing and production tools may be used.

### Human-Led, AI-Assisted

Human authorship remains material, but generative or model-based assistance affected part of the expressive or production process. The public disclosure identifies the category and reviewed scope.

### AI-Generated

A model generated a material expressive element such as melody, lyrics, arrangement, instrumental performance, or synthetic vocal. This category is ineligible for the human-only catalog under the current policy.

### Inconclusive

Evidence is insufficient or conflicting. Inconclusive is not a public accusation.

## Generally allowed under Human-Created

- DAW editing;
- EQ, compression, limiting;
- pitch correction;
- noise reduction;
- time alignment;
- sample libraries used under license;
- MIDI programmed or performed by a person;
- synthesizers controlled by a person;
- restoration;
- non-generative mastering tools.

## Requires disclosure and review

- AI mastering;
- stem separation;
- model-based restoration;
- AI mixing recommendations;
- transformation of human source material;
- assistive lyric or arrangement suggestions not directly included without human authorship;
- voice cleanup using learned models.

## Ineligible for fully human classification

- text-to-music;
- model-generated lyrics presented as human-written;
- model-generated melody/harmony/rhythm;
- generated performance stems;
- synthetic singer presented as a human performance;
- undisclosed voice cloning;
- material generated content with only minor human edits.

## Evidence rubric

Score domains independently:

- identity confidence;
- creation continuity;
- source-file evidence;
- contributor confirmation;
- metadata consistency;
- technical screening;
- external registration consistency;
- review confidence.

Do not collapse the result into one opaque “AI probability.”

## Decision requirements

- no final rejection based solely on detector output;
- manual review for high-risk or disputed cases;
- named certification-standard version;
- clear scope and limitations;
- appeal and re-review process;
- random post-certification audits;
- revocation for material misrepresentation;
- evidence retention policy.

## Public statement

Preferred:

> This recording passed Tourify's Human-Origin review under Standard X.Y. Tourify reviewed the listed identity, declarations, source evidence, contributor confirmations, and technical checks. The certification is not a guarantee that no AI tool was ever used and is not a legal determination of copyright ownership.

The U.S. Copyright Office's current guidance and reports continue to treat human authorship as central to copyrightability of AI-assisted output, so the system must preserve human contribution evidence and avoid simplistic tool-based conclusions.
