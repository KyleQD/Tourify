# Rights Operations, Disputes, and Appeals

## Operations roles

- certification reviewer;
- rights-data reviewer;
- dispute specialist;
- legal administrator;
- security administrator;
- support agent.

Use existing capability checks and least privilege.

## Review queues

- incomplete identity;
- duplicate recording;
- conflicting ISRC;
- claim over-allocation;
- missing contributor;
- evidence anomaly;
- AI-disclosure concern;
- agreement/signature issue;
- external notice;
- appealed decision;
- passport suspension.

## Dispute types

- identity;
- public credit;
- contributor role;
- composition share;
- master ownership;
- administration;
- license;
- sample clearance;
- authority;
- signature validity;
- AI disclosure;
- public display;
- identifier;
- duplicate upload.

## Effects

Depending on severity:

- mark affected claim disputed;
- block passport issuance;
- suspend public badge;
- freeze protected-derivative issuance;
- freeze future financial activation;
- preserve playback if legally safe and policy allows;
- place a narrow public notice;
- preserve all prior evidence.

## Resolution

- unanimous amendment;
- replacement agreement;
- authority confirmation;
- registry correction;
- withdrawal;
- court/arbitration order;
- administrative metadata correction;
- legal escalation.

Tourify Operations cannot adjudicate ownership beyond its policy and evidence procedures.

## Appeals

- defined deadline;
- new evidence upload;
- separate reviewer where practical;
- reasoned decision;
- no retaliation;
- public status remains accurate during review;
- full audit trail.

## Legal notices and DMCA

Integrate with existing reporting/takedown processes. Rights Passport disputes are not a substitute for the DMCA program. A takedown action and a passport-status action should be linked but separately recorded.

## Operational runbooks

- suspicious catalog claim;
- leaked unreleased master;
- compromised reviewer account;
- invalid certificate;
- signing-key compromise;
- blockchain-key compromise;
- false-positive AI concern;
- active contributor dispute;
- urgent court order;
- mass import mismatch.
