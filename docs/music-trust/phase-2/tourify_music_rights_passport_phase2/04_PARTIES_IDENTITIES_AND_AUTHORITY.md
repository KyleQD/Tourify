# Parties, Identities, and Authority

## Party types

- individual;
- stage identity;
- band or informal group;
- legal entity;
- label;
- publisher;
- administrator;
- rights society;
- estate;
- trust;
- authorized representative.

## Identity design

Keep these separate:

- Tourify account;
- legal identity;
- public display identity;
- organization membership;
- authority to act;
- external identifiers.

A user may represent multiple parties, but each authority relationship requires scope and evidence.

## Assurance levels

### Basic

Verified email and Tourify account.

### Confirmed contributor

Phone verification, reauthentication, legal-name declaration, and role confirmation.

### Enhanced

Third-party identity check or equivalent high-assurance process used for higher certification or sensitive signing.

### Organization authority

Entity name, jurisdiction, authority role, effective dates, and supporting evidence. A generic team membership role is not automatically sufficient to transfer or assign rights.

## Invitations

A contributor invitation should contain:

- project and track;
- proposed role;
- proposed claims;
- public-display request;
- required actions;
- expiration;
- invite token bound to recipient and intended party;
- risk warning against sharing the link.

Unregistered contributors may review through a secure claim flow and create an account before signing.

## Authority scopes

Examples:

- confirm own credit;
- confirm own ownership share;
- sign on behalf of organization;
- administer a work;
- approve a master license;
- review evidence;
- act as guardian;
- act as estate representative.

## Revocation and expiry

Authority must be:

- revocable;
- time bounded where appropriate;
- auditable;
- invalidated when organization membership changes;
- rechecked before signing a material amendment.

## Minors

Store minor status privately. Require guardian identity, relationship, authority, consent, and legal-review escalation for long-term grants or transfers. Do not place birth dates or guardian evidence on public manifests.
