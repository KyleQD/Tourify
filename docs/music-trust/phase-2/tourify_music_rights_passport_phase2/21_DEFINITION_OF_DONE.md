# Phase 2 Definition of Done

Phase 2 is complete only when all applicable criteria have evidence.

## Domain

1. `artist_music` remains canonical.
2. Sound recording and musical work are separate linked entities.
3. Release relationships do not create a second playable catalog.
4. Parties, identities, stage names, and authority are separate.
5. Credits and claims are separate.
6. Claims support exact shares, unknown shares, territory, dates, exclusivity, evidence, status, and supersession.
7. Producer points are represented independently from ownership.
8. cover/remix/sample/leased-beat workflows are supported.

## Collaboration and agreements

9. Contributors can be invited securely.
10. Contributors can accept, counter, reject, or dispute.
11. Organization authority is verified.
12. Agreement templates are versioned.
13. Signed documents are reproducible.
14. Signature evidence is restricted and auditable.
15. Material changes create new versions and re-signing.
16. No admin can silently alter signed rights.

## Human origin

17. Certification standard is versioned.
18. AI-use categories and evidence rubric are implemented.
19. No automatic detector-only rejection.
20. Manual review and appeals work.
21. Public claim language is limited and approved.
22. Certification can be suspended, revoked, and superseded.

## Passport

23. Private and public manifests are separate.
24. Manifests are deterministic.
25. Version chain verifies.
26. Issuer signature verifies.
27. VC-compatible credential is generated or the ADR documents the selected equivalent.
28. Credential status supports active, suspended, revoked, and superseded.
29. Public verification reveals no private data.
30. Legacy release and certification dates are accurate.

## Protection

31. Clean master remains private and unchanged.
32. Protected derivatives are traceable to source and recipe.
33. C2PA manifests validate for the supported format matrix.
34. Rights-reservation policy is published.
35. Watermark beta has quality and robustness evidence.
36. Monitoring matches require human review.
37. No guarantee of impossible training is made.
38. Adversarial audio is not in production.

## Blockchain

39. Testnet registry is minimal and nonfinancial.
40. No PII is on-chain.
41. access roles and multisig are configured;
42. anchoring is asynchronous and idempotent;
43. pending/failure does not invalidate the passport;
44. suspend/revoke/supersede events verify;
45. contract source and deployment evidence are recorded.

## Security and operations

46. RLS prevents cross-account access.
47. Evidence and agreements use private storage.
48. Key rotation and compromise runbooks exist.
49. Audit logs are append-only or equivalently protected.
50. DMCA and rights disputes remain separately operable.
51. Backup and restore are tested.
52. security review has no critical unresolved findings;
53. baseline upload/playback/marketplace/share/mobile tests pass;
54. feature flags off restore the pre-Phase-2 user experience;
55. pilot artists complete end to end;
56. the execution JSON contains acceptance evidence for every P0/P1 task.

## Phase 3 readiness

57. Issued passport events are versioned.
58. Future services do not need direct access to draft claims.
59. disputed or suspended interests expose a freeze signal;
60. no financial-token assumptions contaminate the rights model.
