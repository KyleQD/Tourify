# Phase 19 — Global Creator Treaty-System Legacy and Civilizational Continuity Handoff

## Purpose

Define only a future handoff after Phase 18 proves repeated renewal, lawful sunset, archive survival, technology migration, leadership succession, local exit and complete operation without Tourify.

## Potential scope

- century-scale cultural and institutional memory;
- preservation across successor legal orders and extinct technologies;
- successor archives and trusted memory institutions;
- cultural and linguistic continuity of creator records;
- very-long-term identifier and protocol resolution;
- stewardship after dissolution of the originating institution;
- ethical limits on preserving, revealing or reusing sensitive historical records.

## Boundary

No Phase 19 capability ships under Phase 18 flags. Phase 19 cannot claim perpetual legal authority, represent future persons, override privacy or creator rights, create universal identity, adjudicate ownership or prevent lawful local exit.

## Exit criteria

A separate approval package must prove century-scale preservation strategy, successor-custody authority, cultural and linguistic governance, privacy and archival analysis, open specifications, multiple independent archives, sustainable funding, disaster recovery, provider independence and public legitimacy.
