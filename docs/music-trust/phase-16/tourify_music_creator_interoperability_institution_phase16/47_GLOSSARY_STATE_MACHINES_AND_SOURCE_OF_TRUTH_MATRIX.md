# Glossary, State Machines and Source-of-Truth Matrix

## Core terms

### authentic text
A language version designated by the effective instrument as equally authoritative.

### competent authority
A person or organ legally empowered to bind or act for a participant for the exact matter.

### constitutive instrument
The effective legal instrument establishing an organization and defining its powers, organs and membership.

### depositary
A legally appointed custodian performing the functions defined by the applicable instrument and law.

### international organization
A status determined by constitutive basis and applicable law, not a software label.

### member state
A state whose membership is effective under the constitutive instrument after the required national and institutional action.

### public projection
A purpose-limited, minimal view generated from approved internal records.

### privilege schedule
A jurisdiction- and beneficiary-specific record of legally effective privileges or immunities.

### protocol
A separate instrument linked to a constitutive or convention framework with its own participation and entry-into-force rules.

### relationship agreement
An agreement defining cooperation or institutional relationship without automatically transferring legal powers.

### specialized agency
An agency meeting the UN Charter relationship requirements and actually brought into relationship through the applicable process.

### treaty registration
External registration and publication under Article 102 and applicable UN regulations.

## Source-of-truth precedence

1. Effective constitutive instrument or protocol and competent participant authority.
2. Effective governing-body decision, host agreement, privilege schedule and budget appropriation.
3. Official external status from a depositary, government, international organization, court or registry within its own domain.
4. Tourify synchronized operational record and immutable evidence manifest.
5. Approved public projection.

A lower source cannot override a higher source. Conflicts create a hold, dispute flag and review case.

## Common state machines

- Instrument: `draft → negotiated → adopted → signed → ratified_or_approved → effective → amended_or_superseded → withdrawn_or_terminated`.
- Participation: `applied → authority_review → approved → instrument_pending → effective → suspended → withdrawn_or_expired`.
- Service: `designed → legal_review → approved → sandbox → pilot → limited_production → suspended → retired`.
- Privilege: `not_applicable → proposed → legally_granted → effective → waived_or_suspended → expired_or_revoked`.
- Compliance: `report_due → submitted → validated → reviewed → finding → corrective_action → verified_closed_or_escalated`.
- Incident: `detected → contained → authority_review → notified → remediated → independent_review → closed`.
