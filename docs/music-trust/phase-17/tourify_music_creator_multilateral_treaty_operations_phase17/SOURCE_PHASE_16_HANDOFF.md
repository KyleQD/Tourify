# Phase 17 — Global Creator Multilateral Treaty Operations and Periodic Review Handoff

## Purpose

Define a future handoff only after Phase 16 has years of lawful public treaty-system operation, functioning protocols, independent operators, audited funds, effective remedies, public reporting and successful institutional succession drills.

## Potential scope

- mature treaty-protocol administration;
- periodic review conferences and institutional reform;
- additional relationship agreements;
- global implementation and technical-assistance programs;
- long-term public service obligations;
- independent evaluation of creator outcomes;
- orderly protocol amendment, consolidation, suspension and termination.

## Boundary

No Phase 17 capability ships under Phase 16 flags. Phase 17 cannot expand the institution’s competence by software default, convert observer participation into membership, create global creator representation, or adjudicate rights ownership.

## Exit criteria

A separate package must document multi-year performance, participant mandates, legal review, periodic-review authority, funding, host and relationship arrangements, independent accountability, service continuity, public consultation and production architecture.
