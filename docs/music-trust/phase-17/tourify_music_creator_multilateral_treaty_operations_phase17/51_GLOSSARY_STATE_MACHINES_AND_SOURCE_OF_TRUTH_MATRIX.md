# Glossary, State Machines and Source-of-Truth Matrix

## Core distinctions

- **Adopted:** approved by the competent body; not necessarily effective.
- **Effective:** legally or institutionally operative for the specified participants and date.
- **Registered:** accepted into an external registration system; not the same as entry into force.
- **Review:** evidence and assessment process; not an amendment by itself.
- **Amendment:** formal change under the applicable instrument.
- **Protocol:** separate or supplementary instrument with its own participation and effect rules.
- **Annex:** attached material whose amendment process depends on the governing instrument.
- **Suspension:** temporary non-operation of specified obligations or services.
- **Termination:** end of legal operation according to the applicable instrument.
- **Supersession:** replacement by a later version while preserving historical meaning.
- **Interpretive guidance:** labeled explanation; not adjudication or automatic amendment.

## Source priority

1. Current authoritative external official record within its domain.
2. Effective constitutive instrument, protocol, annex and valid participant instrument.
3. Current governing-body decision within competence.
4. Current participant authority and exact-scope mandate.
5. Reconciled Phase 16/17 operational mirror.
6. Approved public projection.
7. Cached credential, identifier document or search index, which never authorizes high-impact action.

## Review-cycle states

`planned → mandate_proposed → mandate_approved → evidence_collection → expert_dialogue → public_consultation → conference → outcome_adopted → implementation → follow_up → closed`

Any state may move to `paused`, `blocked` or `terminated` only through an authorized, recorded transition. Historical cycles are immutable except for versioned corrections.
