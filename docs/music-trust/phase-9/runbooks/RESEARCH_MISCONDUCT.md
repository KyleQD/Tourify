# Runbook — Research Misconduct

## When
Protocol violation, unauthorized export, recommendation language in outputs, or ethics breach.

## Actions
1. Execute `research_misconduct_stop`.
2. Set affected projects to `suspended` / outputs to `revoked`.
3. Block external licensing and clean-room flags.
4. Notify ethics/IRB partner and competition counsel as needed.
5. Do not re-enable until dual-control approval.
