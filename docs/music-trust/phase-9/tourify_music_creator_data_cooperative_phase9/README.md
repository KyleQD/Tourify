# Tourify Creator Data Cooperative and Global Policy Infrastructure Readiness — Phase 9

Phase 9 extends Tourify's creator-rights intelligence foundation into readiness for a separately governed creator data cooperative, licensed research exchange, global policy observatory, standards participation and future approved collective entities.

## Launch boundary

Tourify remains the platform and technical service provider. Cooperative membership, data contribution, voting, research participation, benefit rights, representation and collective licensing require separate explicit records and cannot be inherited from a Tourify account, subscription, upload, certification, Rights Passport or Phase 8 consent.

## First releasable product

The first releasable product is limited to:

1. creator education;
2. cooperative-readiness information;
3. voluntary membership applications for an approved separate entity;
4. granular data-contribution controls;
5. internal, historical, aggregate research using approved extracts; and
6. a sourced policy observatory.

External research licensing, benefit distributions, public research APIs, AI dataset licensing, collective representation, collective licensing, bargaining, coordinated advocacy, transferable membership and tokenization remain disabled until their own approval gates pass.
