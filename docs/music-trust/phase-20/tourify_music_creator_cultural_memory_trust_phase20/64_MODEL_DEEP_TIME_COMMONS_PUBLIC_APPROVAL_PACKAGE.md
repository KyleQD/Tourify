# Model deep-time commons public approval package

This document is a package structure, not a legal instrument or approval.

## 1. Executive decision

- Requested capability and exact scope
- Jurisdictions
- Participants and communities
- Custodians and operators
- Start, review and sunset dates
- Decision owner and independent reviewers

## 2. Legal and entity documents

- Entity-options memorandum
- Formation documents or trust instrument
- Public charter
- Fiduciary, conflict and asset-lock provisions
- Tax and charitable-status analysis
- Insurance, liability and claims plan
- Dissolution and successor-custody plan

## 3. Governance documents

- Participant and creator rights
- Cultural-authority map and recognition process
- Community council charters
- Reserved powers and subsidiarity schedule
- Public consultation report
- Ratification and amendment rules
- Complaints, appeals and remedy matrix
- Anti-capture and independent oversight plan

## 4. Collection and preservation

- Voluntary deposit agreement
- Appraisal and deaccession policy
- Preservation profile and schemas
- Package validation evidence
- Format and technology risk register
- Custodian qualification and independent restore evidence
- Restriction-propagation tests
- Public finding-aid minimization review

## 5. Cultural, privacy and rights safeguards

- Community and Indigenous consultation record
- Sensitive-material classification rules
- Privacy and posthumous-interest assessment
- Copyright and moral-rights analysis
- Repatriation and remediation process
- AI, research and commercial-reuse controls
- Benefit-sharing and output-review policy

## 6. Operations and resilience

- Funding and reserve model
- Procurement and provider contracts
- Staffing and succession plan
- Security and key-management review
- Disaster, conflict and climate plan
- Offline and air-gapped copy strategy
- Provider replacement and Tourify-unavailable results
- Dissolution rehearsal and minimum service floor

## 7. Engineering evidence

- Audited repository paths and deployed objects
- Migrations and generated types
- RLS and storage tests
- Route, worker and webhook tests
- Observability and incident runbooks
- Regression results for every canonical Tourify music and rights path
- Rollback and decommissioning instructions

## 8. External review and public disclosure

- Legal review
- Archival review
- Cultural/community review
- Privacy and security review
- Accessibility and multilingual review
- Independent repository assurance
- Public summary, limitations and unresolved risks

## 9. Activation decision

The final decision must specify approved flags, actors, records, custodians, jurisdictions, traffic limits, expiration, review cadence, monitoring owner, stop conditions and rollback. Anything not expressly approved remains disabled.
