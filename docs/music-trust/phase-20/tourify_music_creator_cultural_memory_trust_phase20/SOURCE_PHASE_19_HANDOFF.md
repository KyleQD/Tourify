# Phase 20 — Global Creator Cultural Memory Trust and Deep-Time Commons Handoff

## Purpose

Define a future handoff only after Phase 19 proves independent multi-archive preservation, successor custody, cultural and linguistic governance, privacy controls, technology reconstruction, post-dissolution stewardship and complete operation without Tourify.

## Potential scope

- a plural, community-governed cultural-memory trust;
- durable public specifications and preservation profiles;
- distributed deep-time custodians across independent legal orders;
- cultural and linguistic councils with exact-scope authority;
- shared preservation funding and emergency response;
- transparent stewardship of identifiers, schemas and public finding aids;
- long-duration access, restriction, repatriation and remediation governance.

## Boundary

No Phase 20 capability ships under Phase 19 flags. A cultural-memory trust cannot claim perpetual legal authority, universal identity, copyright ownership, global representation, compulsory deposit, unrestricted public access or the right to ignore community, privacy, creator or local-sovereignty decisions.

## Required approval package

Phase 20 requires a separate legal structure, public charter, community-governance framework, cultural authority map, privacy and archival analysis, independent funding, multiple successor institutions, open specifications, disaster and dissolution plans, external review, public consultation and production architecture.

## Source-of-truth rules

`artist_music`, Rights Passports, licences, administration cases, royalty ledgers and official external records remain authoritative. Deep-time commons records express preservation, custody, context, access and public projections; they do not rewrite rights, mandates or historical source records.

## Completion evidence

The Phase 20 handoff is complete only when Phase 19 records audited repository paths, deployed objects, preservation and custody evidence, community and privacy approvals, independent archive restores, provider-replacement tests, Tourify-unavailable operation and unresolved blockers in `phase-19-execution-plan.json`.
