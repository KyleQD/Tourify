# Phase 20 definition of done

Phase 20 is not complete because documents exist or code compiles. Completion requires evidence that the selected capability is legally, culturally, technically and operationally authorized for its exact scope.

## Package-level completion

- The repository and deployed Supabase schema are audited and pinned to a commit and environment.
- `phase-20-execution-plan.json` contains all 408 tasks with accurate status, dependencies, files changed, commands, tests, evidence and blockers.
- The Phase 20 legal structure and public charter are approved for the exact pilot jurisdiction and scope.
- Trust participation and deposit are explicitly separate from Tourify accounts and prior-phase participation.
- Cultural-authority methodology, community governance, conflicts, reserved powers, appeals and withdrawal are approved.
- At least two materially independent custodians and two independent implementations are qualified.
- Funding, procurement, staffing, insurance, disaster recovery and dissolution arrangements are approved.

## Source-of-truth and rights safety

- `artist_music` remains the canonical catalog anchor.
- No canonical source record is replaced or mutated by a preservation, access or trust workflow.
- Rights Passports, licences, administration cases, royalty ledgers and official external records remain authoritative.
- Public finding aids are minimal, source-linked, freshness-labeled and non-authorizing.
- Copyright, licensing, payment, enforcement and representation are never inferred from deposit, custody, age, identifiers or credentials.

## Data and security

- All migrations are additive and reviewed against the deployed schema.
- Every new table has explicit RLS and grant tests.
- Restricted evidence is separated from public projections.
- Signed URLs are short-lived and purpose-scoped.
- Outbox workers are idempotent, replay-safe, reconciled and observable.
- Key rotation, compromise, revocation, audit immutability and emergency suspension are tested.

## Preservation and custody

- Preservation packages validate under the pinned Phase 20 profile.
- Source and preservation manifests, fixity, provenance, representation information, significant properties and restrictions are complete.
- Original files and required derivatives are preserved without changing source authority.
- Independent export, acceptance, restore and reconciliation are proven.
- Restriction, community-authority and privacy metadata survive every transfer and restore.

## Access, reuse and remedy

- Public, registered, mediated, research-controlled, community-controlled, sealed and closed access paths are tested.
- Embargo dates trigger review rather than automatic release.
- Access denials provide accessible reasons and appeal.
- Repatriation, shared custody, correction and historical-harm remediation workflows are tested.
- AI training, embeddings, voice/style modeling, bulk extraction and commercial reuse fail closed without explicit authority.
- Benefit and output-review controls are tested where reuse is approved.

## Continuity and regression

- Custodian outage, provider replacement, domain/key loss, network partition and disaster exercises pass.
- Dissolution and post-trust service-floor exercises pass.
- The system operates and restores while Tourify is unavailable.
- Upload, streaming, access, Jukebox, mobile, marketplace, feed, profile, EPK, analytics, licensing, royalties and rights-administration regressions remain green.

## Activation evidence

- The activation scope, jurisdictions, custodians, participants, expiration date, monitoring owner and rollback plan are named.
- Independent legal, cultural, archival, privacy, security and accessibility reviews are attached.
- Public consultation and governing-body decisions are recorded.
- No unresolved critical blocker remains.
- Any item that is proposed, simulated, draft, pending or externally unrecognized is labeled accurately and is not represented as effective.
