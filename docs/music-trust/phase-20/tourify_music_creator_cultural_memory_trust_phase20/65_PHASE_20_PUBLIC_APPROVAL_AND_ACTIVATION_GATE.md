# Phase 20 public approval and activation gate

## Default result

`DENY`

Phase 20 remains disabled until every required item below is current, evidenced and approved for the exact capability and jurisdiction.

## Legal and governance gate

- [ ] A valid legal structure exists for the exact scope.
- [ ] The public charter is effective and accurately published.
- [ ] Tourify is contractually and operationally an optional provider.
- [ ] Participation and deposit are voluntary and separately executed.
- [ ] Cultural-authority recognition, conflicts and reserved powers are operational.
- [ ] Complaints, appeals, correction, withdrawal and repatriation remedies are staffed.
- [ ] Charter amendment, sunset, dissolution and successor-custody rules are effective.

## Custody and preservation gate

- [ ] At least two materially independent custodians are approved.
- [ ] At least two independent implementations validate the pinned profile.
- [ ] Source and preservation manifests pass validation.
- [ ] Fixity, provenance, representation information and restriction metadata are complete.
- [ ] Independent export, acceptance, restore and reconciliation pass.
- [ ] Format, cryptographic and identifier migration plans are current.
- [ ] Restriction propagation survives transfer and restore.

## Privacy, culture and rights gate

- [ ] Creator and community authority is valid for every included collection.
- [ ] Sensitive, sacred, secret and culturally restricted material remains fail-closed.
- [ ] Privacy, posthumous, descendant and affected-person assessments are complete.
- [ ] Copyright, moral-rights and archival-use analysis is complete.
- [ ] Public finding aids pass data-minimization and leakage review.
- [ ] AI, model, research and commercial reuse remain disabled unless separately approved.
- [ ] Access, denial, appeal, repatriation and remediation workflows pass.

## Security, resilience and finance gate

- [ ] RLS, grants, storage and API authorization pass for every role.
- [ ] Critical keys use separation of duties and tested rotation.
- [ ] Provider, funder, cloud and jurisdiction concentration are within policy.
- [ ] Funding, reserves, staffing, insurance and emergency response are approved.
- [ ] Disaster, conflict, network partition and archive-loss exercises pass.
- [ ] Provider replacement, dissolution survival and Tourify-unavailable operation pass.

## Canonical regression gate

- [ ] Existing upload and catalog behavior is unchanged.
- [ ] Streaming, access resolution and Jukebox remain green.
- [ ] Mobile playback remains green.
- [ ] Marketplace, feed, profile, EPK and analytics remain green.
- [ ] Licensing, royalties, Rights Passports and administration remain green.
- [ ] No source-of-truth record is replaced or mutated.

## Activation record

An approval must record:

- exact feature flags;
- exact participants and custodians;
- exact collections and permitted purposes;
- jurisdictions;
- effective and expiry dates;
- traffic and volume limits;
- operational and incident owners;
- monitoring and public-reporting obligations;
- stop conditions;
- rollback and decommissioning;
- unresolved non-critical risks;
- approving authorities and independent reviewers.

Any missing, disputed, expired, stale or simulated item keeps the result at `DENY`.
