# Phase 21 — Global Creator Memory Constellation and Planetary Cultural Resilience Handoff

## Purpose

Define a future handoff only after Phase 20 proves several independent cultural-memory trusts, plural community governance, distributed custodians, open preservation specifications, lawful repatriation, restriction propagation, disaster recovery, dissolution survival and complete operation without Tourify.

## Potential scope

- federation of independently governed cultural-memory trusts;
- cross-trust emergency preservation assistance;
- catastrophic-risk and climate-resilience coordination;
- portable but community-controlled custody and restriction profiles;
- global preservation-capacity mapping and mutual aid;
- deep-time physical, orbital or geographically remote preservation research only through separate scientific and legal approval;
- shared public-interest funding without centralized cultural authority.

## Boundary

No Phase 21 capability ships under Phase 20 flags. A memory constellation cannot become a universal archive, global rights authority, compulsory deposit system, universal identity provider, cultural adjudicator or mechanism to override creator, community, Indigenous, local or national authority.

## Required approval package

Phase 21 requires multiple legally independent trusts, public charters, community authority maps, cross-trust agreements, disaster and conflict analysis, scientific review, privacy and cultural safeguards, funding, provider independence, public consultation and a new production architecture.

## Completion evidence

The Phase 21 handoff is complete only when Phase 20 records audited repository paths, deployed objects, charter and governance evidence, cultural approvals, independent custodian restores, mutual-aid drills, dissolution survival, Tourify-unavailable operation and unresolved blockers in `phase-20-execution-plan.json`.
