# Tourify Global Creator Digital Commons and Multilateral Stewardship — Phase 12

Phase 12 extends Phase 11 public-infrastructure readiness into durable, multilateral stewardship of open protocols, independent registries, public assets, conformance, operations and continuity that can survive Tourify’s withdrawal or failure.

## Launch boundary

Tourify remains an optional implementation and service provider. Production commons infrastructure requires a separately approved steward, public governance, neutral asset custody, diversified funding, two or more independent implementations, operator-replacement drills and public-interest review. A Tourify account or prior-phase participation does not create commons membership, public identity, data contribution, ownership transfer, representation or collective-action authority.

## First releasable product

The first release is limited to:

1. Phase 12 readiness education and public asset inventory;
2. a separately approved sandbox steward and charter;
3. neutral custody or escrow plans for domains, marks, repositories and signing material;
4. sandbox registry projections and rights-reference resolution;
5. two independent protocol implementations and operators;
6. conformance and portability testing;
7. full operator-replacement and Tourify-unavailable drills; and
8. a public approval package for narrowly scoped limited production.

Universal identity, global mandates, collective action, compulsory enrollment, irreversible asset transfer and production critical infrastructure remain disabled.
