# Tourify Global Rights Intelligence and Collective Negotiation Readiness — Phase 8

Phase 8 extends Tourify’s verified catalog and rights-administration ecosystem into privacy-preserving creator intelligence, historical operational benchmarks, policy and contract education, and legally separated negotiation-readiness workflows.

## Launch boundary

Tourify begins as the software, data-governance, benchmark, education and facilitation layer. It does not become a union, CMO, trade association, rate bureau, bargaining representative, licensing collective or attorney by implication. Any real representation, collective licensing or bargaining requires a separate approved entity, mandate, counsel review and jurisdiction plan.
