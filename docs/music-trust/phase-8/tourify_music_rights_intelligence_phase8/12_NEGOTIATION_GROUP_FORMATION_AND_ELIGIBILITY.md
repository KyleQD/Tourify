# Negotiation Group Formation and Eligibility

Negotiation groups begin as governance and readiness objects, not as entities authorized to bargain or license.

## Group types

Education cohort, research consortium, policy advocacy group, labor-organizing group, collective-licensing feasibility group and authorized representative group must be distinct.

## Eligibility

Rights type, role, territory, employment/contractor status, counterparty relationship, conflicts, active authority, jurisdiction and counsel approval.

## Participation

Voluntary opt-in, independent exit, no retaliation, no effect on other Tourify services, and no disclosure of membership where confidential.

## Formation package

Purpose, permitted topics, prohibited topics, entity status, counsel, facilitator, funding, governance, confidentiality, term, complaint path and dissolution.

## Default state

`readiness_only`. External negotiation, proposals, pricing discussions and representation are disabled until separate approval gates pass.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence

Codex must record audited repository paths, deployed database objects, migrations, RLS tests, route tests, privacy and competition reviews, feature flags, monitoring, rollback instructions and task-level evidence in `phase-8-execution-plan.json`.
