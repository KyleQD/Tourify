# Group Governance, Voting and Ratification

Governance must distinguish nonbinding research preferences from decisions that an authorized entity may act upon.

## Governance model

Membership classes, quorum, voting weights, conflicts, recusals, proposal rights, amendment thresholds, emergency suspension, records and dissolution.

## Proposal classes

Educational priority, policy position, research question, governance rule, non-price contract principle, counsel-approved bargaining proposal and final agreement ratification.

## Voting privacy

Secret ballots where appropriate; do not reveal individual commercial positions to other participants or counterparties.

## Ratification

A group vote does not bind an artist unless the governing documents and active mandate legally provide that result and counsel confirms it.

## Minority rights

Exit, dissent record, non-retaliation, access to final terms and complaint/appeal process.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.
