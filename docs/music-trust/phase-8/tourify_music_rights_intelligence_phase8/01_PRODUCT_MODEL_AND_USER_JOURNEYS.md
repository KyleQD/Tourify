# Product Model and User Journeys

Phase 8 should feel like an artist intelligence center rather than a collective-pricing engine.

## Artist journey

An artist opts into defined intelligence purposes, receives private catalog diagnostics, compares results to privacy-approved cohorts, sees educational explanations of contract terms and policy changes, and may later join a counsel-approved readiness group. Participation never changes discovery ranking, licensing access or enforcement support.

## Manager, label and administrator journey

Authorized organizations may view portfolio intelligence only for assets and parties within their active authority. Cross-client benchmarking requires separate consent and aggregation. Raw client terms cannot be shared across portfolio companies.

## Research and policy journey

Approved analysts monitor official policy sources, create versioned summaries, attach confidence and expiry dates, and publish educational alerts after legal/editorial review.

## Negotiation-readiness journey

Participants define common non-price issues, review public or properly aggregated evidence, vote on governance rules and decide independently whether to support a proposal. External delivery remains disabled until counsel approves the group, entity, mandate and communication.

## Public output tiers

- Private individual insight.
- Restricted cohort administrator output.
- Published aggregate benchmark.
- Educational policy alert.
- Counsel-approved group position.
- Separately authorized representation or licence offer.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence

Codex must record audited repository paths, deployed database objects, migrations, RLS tests, route tests, privacy and competition reviews, feature flags, monitoring, rollback instructions and task-level evidence in `phase-8-execution-plan.json`.
