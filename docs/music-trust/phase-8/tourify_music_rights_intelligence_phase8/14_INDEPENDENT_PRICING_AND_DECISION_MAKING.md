# Independent Pricing and Decision-Making

Tourify must preserve each artist’s independent commercial decisions even when providing data or group-readiness tools.

## Product rules

No recommended price, floor, ceiling, discount, commission or refusal. No “artists like you charge” output using recent confidential data.

## Decision separation

Benchmarks are viewed privately; Tourify does not share whether a participant adopted a price or term. Group votes cannot bind independent transactions without a separately authorized representative structure.

## Algorithm controls

Pricing, offer and contract tools cannot optimize toward uniform terms across competitors. Models must not use competitor nonpublic decisions as target labels.

## Communication controls

No participant directory with current rate cards or negotiation status. Direct messages in a group workspace are monitored according to approved policy and retention.

## Audit tests

Search outputs, exports, prompts and discussion logs for prohibited recommendations, rate-floor language, coordinated refusals and named competitor comparisons.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence

Codex must record audited repository paths, deployed database objects, migrations, RLS tests, route tests, privacy and competition reviews, feature flags, monitoring, rollback instructions and task-level evidence in `phase-8-execution-plan.json`.
