# Antitrust, Labor and Competition Guardrails

Because the federal competitor-collaboration guidelines were withdrawn and updated guidance was still under consideration in 2026, Phase 8 must use matter-specific counsel rather than relying on a generic safety-zone claim.

## Prohibited conduct

Price fixing, coordinated minimums, bid rigging, market division, group boycotts, output restrictions, exchange of current/future competitively sensitive information, and algorithmic coordination.

## Sensitive topics filter

Block or interrupt discussions involving specific future rates, named counterparties, planned release/output, pending bids, customer allocation, discounts, individual costs and retaliation.

## Labor analysis

Determine whether participants are employees, independent contractors, businesses, licensors or mixed roles; identify the counterparty and whether the activity concerns labor conditions or sale/licensing of independent intellectual property.

## Counsel controls

Counsel-approved agenda, trained facilitator, attendance record, warnings, real-time topic intervention, minutes, escalation and preservation.

## Jurisdiction matrix

Federal, state and international labor and competition rules may differ. No group is activated globally from one U.S. memo.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence

Codex must record audited repository paths, deployed database objects, migrations, RLS tests, route tests, privacy and competition reviews, feature flags, monitoring, rollback instructions and task-level evidence in `phase-8-execution-plan.json`.
