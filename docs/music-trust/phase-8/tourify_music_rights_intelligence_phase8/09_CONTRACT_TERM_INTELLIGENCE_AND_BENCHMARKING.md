# Contract-Term Intelligence and Benchmarking

Contract intelligence should explain clauses and historical distributions without exposing confidential counterparties or prescribing terms.

## Term taxonomy

Grant scope, term, territory, exclusivity, options, reversion, accounting, reserves, audit, recoupment, commissions, approvals, MFN, indemnity, warranties, AI rights, data rights and dispute resolution.

## Extraction

Machine extraction creates suggestions with page references and confidence. A user or reviewer confirms each structured term before it enters intelligence datasets.

## Benchmark output

Use ranges, prevalence and historical frequencies only after privacy and competition gates. Do not show a user exactly what named peers accepted.

## Educational language

Explain common effects and questions to consider, not whether a clause is legally valid, fair or should be accepted.

## Confidentiality

Respect agreement confidentiality, trade-secret restrictions, privilege and data-use limitations. A participant cannot contribute a contract it lacks authority to analyze.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence
