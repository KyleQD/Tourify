# Phase 8 Scope, Dependencies and Boundaries

Phase 8 creates a governed rights-intelligence and negotiation-readiness layer over consented, quality-controlled outputs from Phases 1–7. It begins with education and aggregate benchmarking. It does not begin with coordinated negotiations, rate-setting, collective licensing, representation or external demands.

## Required prerequisites

Phase 7 must prove consent lineage, mandate scope, official-source reconciliation, data-quality scoring, dispute handling, auditability, opt-out propagation and operational ownership. Phase 8 cannot use raw enforcement allegations, estimated recoveries or unverified claims as benchmark facts.

## In scope

- Versioned intelligence consent and purpose controls.
- Privacy-preserving aggregate statistics and cohorts.
- Policy, contract-term and rights-risk education.
- Creator dashboards and alerts.
- Negotiation-readiness workspaces that do not recommend price.
- Separate approval packages for any representative, labor, collective-licensing or group-negotiation activity.

## Out of scope by default

- Price recommendations, rate floors or standardized commercial terms.
- Automated legal or contract advice.
- Joint refusals to deal or retaliation.
- Individual competitor data sharing.
- CMO, union, trade association, bargaining representative or licensing-agent status by implication.
- Automated external communications on behalf of a group.

## Launch sequence

1. Audit and governance.
2. Consent and data-purpose registry.
3. Private creator intelligence.
4. Educational aggregate benchmark pilot.
5. Contract and policy education.
6. Negotiation-readiness simulation.
7. Separate legal approval before any real group representation or licensing.

## Existing-system integration

- Consume versioned Phase 7 events, reconciled official-source mirrors and approved aggregate extracts.
- Never mutate source administration, claim, enforcement, licence, payment or Rights Passport records from an intelligence workflow.
- Keep upload, playback, entitlement, marketplace, profile, feed, EPK, analytics and mobile behavior unchanged unless a separately tested display adapter is added.

## Stop conditions

Stop publication or negotiation activity when consent is invalid, a cohort is too small, one participant dominates the statistic, source data is stale, re-identification risk is unacceptable, competitively sensitive information may be exposed, legal approval is missing, or an output could be understood as a coordinated price or legal instruction.

## Completion evidence

Codex must record audited repository paths, deployed database objects, migrations, RLS tests, route tests, privacy and competition reviews, feature flags, monitoring, rollback instructions and task-level evidence in `phase-8-execution-plan.json`.
