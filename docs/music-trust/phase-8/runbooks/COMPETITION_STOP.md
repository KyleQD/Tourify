# Runbook — Competition Stop

## When
Any signal of coordinated pricing, rate floors, boycotts, market allocation, or recommendation language in outputs/groups.

## Actions
1. Execute admin `competition_stop` (or disable benchmarks/groups/public publish/external/collective flags).
2. Block/suppress proposals matching antitrust topic screens.
3. Confirm negotiation groups remain `external_action_enabled=false` and `state=readiness_only` (or dissolved).
4. Notify competition counsel; freeze methodology releases under review.
5. Preserve proposal/topic_screen evidence.

## Hard rule
Tourify must never become a rate bureau or bargaining representative by implication.
