# Tourify Global Creator Protocol Commons and Constitutional Stewardship — Phase 13

Phase 13 extends the independently operable Phase 12 digital commons into long-term constitutional stewardship: entrenched public-interest protections, compact ratification, local reserved powers, open protocol rights, amendment classification, formal objections, independent review, succession, legitimate forks, multi-root trust and durable public accountability.

## Launch boundary

Tourify remains an optional implementation and service provider. A Tourify account or prior-phase participation does not create compact membership, constitutional authority, public identity, data contribution, rights ownership, representation, licensing, collection, bargaining or enforcement authority.

The Phase 13 compact is a separately approved contractual and governance instrument. It must not be described as a treaty, court, regulator, CMO or international organization unless an independently formed and legally qualified body actually has that status.
