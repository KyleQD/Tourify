# Build a Living EPK: How Tourify Helps Artists Stay Ready for Opportunities

**By Tourify**

An artist’s electronic press kit should make it easy for a promoter, venue, journalist, buyer, or potential collaborator to understand the project. Too often, however, an EPK becomes a collection of outdated attachments, broken links, old streaming numbers, and multiple versions of the same biography.

Tourify’s EPK and artist-profile tools are designed to replace that scattered process with a living, shareable destination.

## What is a living EPK?

A traditional electronic press kit usually includes an artist biography, music, photos, videos, press coverage, contact information, and performance history. A living EPK includes those essentials while remaining connected to the artist’s current Tourify profile and available data.

Instead of rebuilding a PDF every time something changes, an artist can update the information at its source. A new tour date, featured release, photo, video, or press mention can be reflected in the profile and EPK without sending an entirely new package to every contact.

Berklee Online recommends including an EPK or one-sheet when approaching venues because it gives promoters a practical way to hear the music and review an artist’s performance background. The easier that information is to evaluate, the easier it is for an industry contact to decide whether the artist is a fit.

## What artists can include

A Tourify EPK can organize the most important parts of an artist’s professional identity, including:

- Artist biography and short professional summary.
- Featured songs, releases, and music-player content.
- Photos, videos, and approved promotional media.
- Upcoming and past performance dates.
- Press quotes, coverage, and notable achievements.
- Audience, social, or streaming insights when supported integrations are connected.
- Booking, management, publicity, or general contact information.
- Technical, hospitality, and performance information where applicable.
- Links to tickets, merchandise, websites, and external platforms.

Artists can present this information through a customizable template that matches the visual identity of the project.

## Why connected analytics matter

Streaming and audience analytics help artists understand where listeners are located, which songs are connecting, and how engagement changes over time. Spotify for Artists describes its data as a way to understand who and where an audience is and which songs are connecting most strongly. Apple Music for Artists similarly provides performance information such as plays, listeners, Shazam activity, purchases, and radio spins.

Tourify’s role is to help artists bring useful parts of that broader professional picture into one ecosystem. The goal is not to replace each streaming service’s analytics dashboard. It is to make an artist’s Tourify presence more current, credible, and useful.

## Benefits for artists

A living EPK can help artists:

- Send one professional link instead of several attachments.
- Reduce the risk of sharing outdated information.
- Present music, media, events, and professional details together.
- Create a stronger first impression with promoters and media contacts.
- Maintain different visual styles without rebuilding the underlying content.
- Make booking and press information easier to find.
- Use connected data to support pitches and planning.

The same profile can also serve fans, collaborators, venues, and potential team members, while the EPK view focuses the information for professional use.

## How to access the EPK builder

1. Sign in and select or create an Artist account.
2. Open the Artist Dashboard.
3. Navigate to the profile, appearance, or EPK section.
4. Complete the core artist information, including biography, location, genres, contact details, and featured media.
5. Add music, videos, photos, events, press coverage, and professional documents.
6. Connect supported external services when integrations are available.
7. Select an EPK template and adjust the available appearance settings.
8. Preview the public version on desktop and mobile.
9. Publish the EPK and copy the shareable link.

Before sending the link, confirm that the featured music works, contact information is current, and the strongest material appears first.

## Always ready for the next opportunity

Opportunities often arrive quickly. A venue may need a support act. A journalist may request approved images. A festival buyer may want performance history. A potential manager may want to review audience development.

A living Tourify EPK helps artists respond with a professional, current presentation instead of assembling materials at the last minute. It turns the artist profile into an active business tool—one that grows alongside the career it represents.
