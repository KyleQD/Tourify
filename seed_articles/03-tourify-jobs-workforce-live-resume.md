# From Gig Search to Live Résumé: Tourify’s Jobs and Workforce Ecosystem

**By Tourify**

Every live event depends on people whose work may never appear on the marquee. Stagehands, audio engineers, lighting technicians, photographers, runners, security teams, hospitality staff, production managers, drivers, marketers, and many other professionals turn a plan into a real experience.

Tourify’s jobs and workforce ecosystem is designed to connect those workers with opportunities while helping employers manage the complete process—from job posting to onboarding, scheduling, and work history.

## More than a job board

A standard job board usually ends when an application is submitted. Live-event work requires more coordination.

A worker may need to provide certifications, complete forms, accept policies, upload documents, review call times, receive assignments, and communicate with a manager before arriving onsite. Employers may need to compare applicants, approve hires, track onboarding, assign shifts, and confirm that the right people have the right information.

Tourify brings those steps into one connected workflow.

## Tools for workers

General users can present themselves as industry professionals by adding their roles, skills, experience, portfolio material, availability, and other relevant information to their profiles.

Workers can use Tourify to:

- Search for live-event, touring, venue, creative, and production opportunities.
- Apply using an existing professional profile.
- Keep work samples, qualifications, and experience in one place.
- Complete employer-specific onboarding requirements.
- Receive updates about applications, approvals, assignments, and shifts.
- Build a continuing record of completed work through the platform.
- Collect endorsements, badges, or verified experience when supported.

This creates the foundation for a live résumé: a professional history that can develop as a user completes legitimate work through Tourify’s ecosystem.

## Tools for employers

Organizations, venues, artists, and authorized administrators can use workforce tools to:

- Create job listings for specific events, tours, departments, or dates.
- Define required skills, certifications, experience, and availability.
- Review candidates and move them through a structured hiring process.
- Assign the correct onboarding package to each position.
- Track missing forms, documents, and approvals.
- Add hired workers to rosters and teams.
- Schedule shifts, call times, departments, and work zones.
- Maintain a clearer operational record of who is assigned to what.

Structured onboarding is not just paperwork. SHRM’s onboarding guidance emphasizes that quality onboarding supports long-term employee success, productivity, engagement, and retention. In temporary and project-based work, it also helps reduce confusion before the worker arrives.

## The benefits of a connected workflow

For workers, the main benefit is continuity. The information used to apply for a role can remain connected to onboarding, assignments, and completed experience. Users do not need to rebuild their professional identity for every opportunity.

For employers, the benefit is visibility. Hiring status, missing requirements, team assignments, and schedules can be reviewed in a shared system rather than scattered across email threads, spreadsheets, text messages, and paper forms.

A connected workflow can help reduce:

- Duplicate data entry.
- Missed onboarding requirements.
- Confusion about call times or assignments.
- Unclear approval status.
- Incomplete worker records.
- Difficulty confirming prior experience.

## How workers access jobs

1. Create or sign in to a general Tourify account.
2. Complete the professional sections of your profile.
3. Add your industry roles, skills, work history, portfolio, certifications, and availability.
4. Open the Jobs area from the primary navigation.
5. Filter opportunities by role, location, date, organization, or event type.
6. Review the requirements and submit an application.
7. Monitor notifications and application status.
8. Complete any assigned onboarding steps after approval.
9. Review your assignments and schedule once hired.

## How employers access workforce tools

1. Sign in to an eligible Venue, Organization, Artist, or administrative account.
2. Open Jobs, Hiring, Staff, or Workforce from the dashboard.
3. Create a role and connect it to the appropriate event, tour, venue, or organization.
4. Add requirements and select an onboarding template.
5. Publish the opportunity and review applicants.
6. Approve selected candidates and track onboarding completion.
7. Add hired workers to the roster.
8. Create shifts, assignments, and communication updates.

## Building careers through completed work

The live-events industry is highly relationship-driven, but professional relationships become stronger when experience is easier to document and trust.

Tourify’s workforce ecosystem is designed to help people find work, complete the process successfully, and carry the value of that experience forward. Over time, each completed job can contribute to a clearer professional story—one built through real participation in the events and tours that shape the industry.
